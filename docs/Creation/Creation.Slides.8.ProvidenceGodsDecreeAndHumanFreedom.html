# Providence: God's Decree and Human Freedom --- ## Outline * [What Providence Is](#slide3) * [Three Views](#slide7) * [The Scripture](#slide13) * [TULIP and ROSES](#slide22) * [The Revisit](#slide29) * [Molinism at Work in the Series](#slide38) * [Practical Takeaways](#slide40) --- ## Where We Are: God Governs His World ![](pics/HandsEarth.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem") * Deck 1: God created everything out of nothing, **conserves** it in being, and **concurs** with every creaturely act * Now: how does God **govern** all that happens – especially the free choices of people? * Christians have split over this question for centuries – and some marriages nearly did * This deck weighs three rival answers: **Calvinism, Arminianism, Molinism** * All three are held by sincere, Bible-believing Christians – we honor the difference and give the reasons on each side --- # What Providence Is --- ## God Governs All That Happens ![](pics/GodAndWorldDetails.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem") * **Providence**: God's governance or supervision of the world – everything that happens * Scripture teaches a very strong **sovereignty** (D. A. Carson's summary): * God is Creator, Ruler, and Possessor of all things; the ultimate personal cause of all that happens; He elects His people * Scripture equally presupposes human **freedom and responsibility**: * Commands, choices, sin and judgment, testing, rewards, real prayer, God pleading with sinners – and God relenting when people turn * The task: a model that affirms **both** streams, not one at the expense of the other --- ## Ordinary and Extraordinary Providence ![](pics/GodCosmosClock.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem") * **Extraordinary providence**: miracles – God acts apart from secondary natural causes (Deck 9) * **Ordinary providence**: God governs through natural causes and free creatures – the topic of this deck * The question: how does God reach His ends **through** the free decisions of people? * Recall Deck 1: Aquinas – God moves the will; Molina – God and the will pull together, like **two men on two ropes** bringing one boat ashore * That difference in concurrence is exactly the difference between the views ahead --- ## Two Kinds of "Freedom" ![](pics/Dominos.jpg style="float: right; width: 9rem") * **Determinism**: God decides every detail and plans the causes that bring it about * **Compatibilist freedom**: you are causally determined, yet you act **voluntarily** – by your own desires, not against your will * **Libertarian freedom**: you can **really choose otherwise**; your choice is not causally determined – it is up to you * Both sides agree God is **sovereign**: He cannot be overruled * The dispute: does sovereignty **require** determinism? --- # Three Views --- ## Calvinism ![](pics/CalvinFreeWill.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem") * After John Calvin; also called Reformed theology * **Universal divine causal determinism**: God unilaterally determines everything that happens * Freedom is **compatibilist**: God never drags you kicking and screaming – you do what you want, but you cannot do otherwise * Strength: the strongest possible sovereignty – nothing escapes God's plan, all glory goes to God * Salvation is **wholly God's work**, from first to last --- ## Arminianism ![](pics/FreeWillVsDeterminism.jpg style="float: right; width: 11rem") * After Jacob Arminius (Dutch, 17th century) * **Libertarian freedom**: people can do otherwise – so God can justly hold them responsible * Sovereignty through **simple foreknowledge**: God knows what free people **will** do and ordains it accordingly * Foreknowing does not cause: an infallible **barometer** tells you the weather with certainty, but it does not make the weather * Strength: a sincere offer of salvation to all, and a God who is not the author of sin --- ## Molinism ![](pics/ManyWorlds.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem") * After the Jesuit Luis Molina (1535–1600) – Arminius himself seems to have held it * **Libertarian freedom** – like Arminianism * Sovereignty through **middle knowledge**: God knows what every possible person **would** freely do in every possible circumstance * On that basis God decrees which people to create, in which **freedom-permitting** circumstances – and so reaches His ends through free choices * Analogy: an FBI **sting** – no coercion, but they know the dealer well enough to know he would freely sell --- ## Three Moments of God's Knowledge ![](pics/omnicience.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem") * **Natural knowledge** – what **could** be: every logically possible world * **Middle knowledge** – what **would** be: how every free creature would choose in any circumstance * **Free knowledge** – what **will** be: the world God actually decided to create * "Middle" because it sits between the other two – independent of God's will, yet about contingent things * Defended in detail in the [Omniscience deck](../GodAttributes/GodOmniscience.sildes.html#slide24) --- ## The Logical Order ![](pics/ChronoLogical.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem") * Not steps in time – God is not waiting – but **logical** priority, which explains which depends on which * **Calvinism**: God's plan → His foreknowledge; everything happens by His will * **Arminianism**: foreknowledge of what **will** happen → plan; but that knowledge comes only **after** the decree * **Molinism**: natural knowledge → middle knowledge → **decree** → free knowledge * Only Molinism places knowledge of free choices **before** the decree – so the plan can use it --- # The Scripture --- ## He Works All Things ![](pics/Decree.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem")

"In Him also we have obtained an inheritance, being predestined according to the purpose of Him who **works all things according to the counsel of His will**,"

Ephesians 1:11 (NKJV)

"Therefore He has mercy on whom He wills, and whom He wills He hardens."

Romans 9:18 (NKJV)

* **Calvinism**: all things – including every choice – flow from His will alone * **Arminianism**: Romans 9 is about *whom* God saves – Gentiles as well as Jews, all who believe * **Molinism**: "all things" includes the free choices He foresaw and chose to permit --- ## The Cross Was Planned ![](pics/StringsAndScroll.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem")

"For truly against Your holy Servant Jesus, whom You anointed, both Herod and Pontius Pilate, with the Gentiles and the people of Israel, were gathered together to do **whatever Your hand and Your purpose determined before to be done**."

Acts 4:27–28 (NKJV)

* Also: "delivered by the determined purpose and foreknowledge of God" (Acts 2:23) – yet by "lawless hands" * **Calvinism**: God determined even the named individuals' acts * **Arminianism**: God foresaw it – but how did He *plan* it? (see the revisit) --- ## The Golden Chain ![](pics/OrderOfSalvation.jpg style="float: right; width: 11rem")

"For **whom He foreknew**, He also predestined to be conformed to the image of His Son, that He might be the firstborn among many brethren. Moreover whom He predestined, these He also called; whom He called, these He also justified; and whom He justified, these He also glorified."

Romans 8:29–30 (NKJV)

* **Calvinism**: "foreknew" = foreloved (Jeremiah 1:5); an unbroken chain – no one drops out * **Arminianism**: God predestines the **group** in Christ to be conformed – like a tour whose sights are fixed, not its members --- ## Drawn and Appointed ![](pics/GodsHandAndPeolpe.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

"No one can come to Me unless the Father who sent Me **draws** him; and I will raise him up at the last day."

John 6:44 (NKJV)

"[...] And as many as **had been appointed to eternal life** believed."

Acts 13:48 (NKJV)

* **Calvinism**: drawing is effectual; appointment is individual (passive voice: God appoints) * **Arminianism**: drawing is necessary, not irresistible; "disposed to eternal life" --- ## Choose Life ![](pics/GodRoadFork.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

"I call heaven and earth as witnesses today against you, that I have set before you life and death, blessing and cursing; therefore **choose life**, that both you and your descendants may live;"

Deuteronomy 30:19 (NKJV)

"[...] if that nation against whom I have spoken turns from its evil, **I will relent** of the disaster that I thought to bring upon it."

Jeremiah 18:8 (NKJV)

* **Arminianism/Molinism**: real options, real responses * **Calvinism**: "ought" does not imply "can"; God ordains the means too --- ## God Desires All ![](pics/GodOutstretchedArms.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem")

"who **desires all men to be saved** and to come to the knowledge of the truth."

1 Timothy 2:4 (NKJV)

"The Lord is not slack concerning His promise, as some count slackness, but is longsuffering toward us, **not willing that any should perish** but that all should come to repentance."

2 Peter 3:9 (NKJV)

* **Arminianism/Molinism**: a sincere, universal offer * **Calvinism**: "all kinds of men", or God's desire vs His decree --- ## You Always Resist ![](pics/GodChangeMind.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

"You stiff-necked and uncircumcised in heart and ears! **You always resist the Holy Spirit**; as your fathers did, so do you."

Acts 7:51 (NKJV)

"[...] How often **I wanted** to gather your children together, as a hen gathers her chicks under her wings, but **you were not willing**!"

Matthew 23:37 (NKJV)

* **Arminianism/Molinism**: grace can be resisted * **Calvinism**: the general call is resisted; the effectual call is not --- ## Both at Once ![](pics/JosephAndBrothers.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

"But as for you, **you meant evil** against me; but **God meant it for good**, in order to bring it about as it is this day, to save many people alive."

Genesis 50:20 (NKJV)

"[...] **work out your own salvation** with fear and trembling; for **it is God who works in you** both to will and to do for His good pleasure."

Philippians 2:12–13 (NKJV)

* Every view claims these texts – the question is which **model** does them justice --- # TULIP and ROSES --- ## Five Points, Two Flowers ![](pics/TulipsAndRoses.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem") * **TULIP** – the five points of Calvinism (Canons of Dort, 1618–1619, answering the five Arminian articles; the acronym is modern), each providence applied to salvation * **ROSES** – the non-Calvinist answer (Arminianism and Molinism): * **T**otal depravity ↔ **R**adical depravity * **U**nconditional election ↔ **S**overeign election * **L**imited atonement ↔ **S**ufficient atonement * **I**rresistible grace ↔ **O**vercoming grace * **P**erseverance of the saints ↔ **E**nduring of the saints --- ## T – Total Depravity ![](pics/OriginalSin.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem")

"And you He made alive, who were **dead in trespasses and sins**,"

Ephesians 2:1 (NKJV)

* **Calvinist**: fallen man is spiritually dead – unable even to understand or seek the gospel ("total inability") * **Radical depravity**: all are sinners, none come unaided – but **prevenient grace** enables every hearer to respond * Scripture shows seekers before regeneration: Cornelius (Acts 10), Lydia "who worshiped God" (Acts 16:14); Nicodemus understood Jesus --- ## U – Unconditional Election ![](pics/PickOne.png style="float: right; width: 8rem") * **Calvinist**: before creation God chose individuals for salvation for no reason but His will (Ephesians 1:4–5) * **Sovereign election** (Arminian): God sovereignly chose a **people** – all who are **in Christ**; individuals share it by faith * "Chosen" often means set apart for a task – King Saul (1 Samuel 15) and Judas were chosen, yet fell (John 6:70) * **Molinist**: corporate **and** individual – God chose the world in which He knew each of the elect would freely believe * Craig: corporate election fits Romans 9–10, but Acts 13:48 and Paul's call (Galatians 1:15) are individual --- ## L – Limited Atonement ![](pics/ChristWork.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

"And He Himself is the propitiation for our sins, and not for ours only but also **for the whole world**."

1 John 2:2 (NKJV)

* **Calvinist**: Christ died for all sins of **some** men – the elect; His death surely achieves what it intends * **Sufficient atonement**: sufficient for all, **applied** to those who believe * So we can tell any friend: "Jesus died for you" – "4-point" Calvinists agree, though that strains T, U, and I --- ## I – Irresistible Grace ![](pics/Regeneration.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem")

"Therefore, King Agrippa, **I was not disobedient** to the heavenly vision,"

Acts 26:19 (NKJV)

* **Calvinist**: the elect receive an **effectual call**; regeneration comes (logically) **before** faith – like a chain holding up a chandelier * **Overcoming grace**: grace woos and enables, but can be refused; faith → regeneration * Even Paul, chosen before birth, could have been "disobedient" * Faith is no work to boast of (Romans 4:5) – "not of yourselves" (Ephesians 2:8) refers to the whole salvation --- ## P – Perseverance of the Saints ![](pics/Persevere.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

"[...] And I give them eternal life, and they shall never perish; neither shall **anyone snatch them** out of My hand."

John 10:28 (NKJV)

"Beware, brethren, lest there be in any of you an evil heart of unbelief **in departing** from the living God;"

Hebrews 3:12 (NKJV)

* **Calvinist**: God preserves every elect person; **Enduring**: no one snatches us, yet one may walk away * Details: Topic 12.4 "Can we throw away our salvation?" --- # The Revisit --- ## Calvinism: Craig's Five Problems ![](pics/GodsToys.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem") * **Not a coherent reading**: it keeps sovereignty by denying the real contingency the freedom texts affirm – many Reformed theologians admit "mystery" * **Self-defeating**: if determinism is true, you were determined to believe it – not by weighing the arguments * **Author of sin**: God moves people to choose evil and they cannot do otherwise; responsibility disappears * **Only one agent**: people become instruments, like a stick moving a stone * **A farce**: marionettes lecturing marionettes on predestination --- ## Calvinism: The Reply and Its Cost ![](pics/Pinochio.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem") * The Calvinist answer: we act **voluntarily**, so we are responsible; sovereignty must be total * But voluntary is not free: the terrorist's van with the **stuck accelerator** kills either way – his foot made no difference * Why then does God plead, test, reward, and judge as if choices were real? * The honest verdict: Calvinism wins sovereignty but **loses genuine freedom** – one stream of Scripture is sacrificed * What it rightly guards: salvation is grace from first to last – Molinism keeps that too --- ## Arminianism: Two Problems ![](pics/GodSeesFuture.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem") * It keeps freedom – but sovereignty weakens: * **Foreordination becomes a fifth wheel**: if God only ordains what He sees *will* happen, ordaining adds nothing * **No real planning**: before the decree God knows only what *could* happen, not what people *would* do * Then after creating He must think, "What a break! Pilate and Herod did just what they were supposed to" * Worse: He could not even know if Pilate, Herod, or Rome would exist – the cross looks like luck (Acts 4:27–28) --- ## Molinism: The Cross by Plan ![](pics/DivineChess.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem")

"which none of the rulers of this age knew; for **had they known, they would not have crucified** the Lord of glory."

1 Corinthians 2:8 (NKJV)

* God knew what Pilate, Herod, the Sanhedrin, and the crowd **would** freely do in AD 30 – and decreed that world * So everything happened "by the determined purpose and foreknowledge of God", yet by free "lawless hands" * A counterfactual in Scripture itself – and a sovereignty that calls for **adoration** --- ## Molinism: Times and Places ![](pics/EyeOverFork.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem")

"And He has made from one blood every nation of men to dwell on all the face of the earth, and has **determined their preappointed times and the boundaries of their dwellings**, so that they should seek the Lord, in the hope that they might grope for Him and find Him, though He is not far from each one of us;"

Acts 17:26–27 (NKJV)

* God knew Saul **would** obey if Christ appeared on the Damascus road – so He appeared * Election is individual (Romans 8:29) yet fully free --- ## Objection: Too Much Control? ![](pics/ScaleFreeAndGuided.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem") * Reformed objection: nudge one molecule on Alpha Centauri and P would choose differently – so God gets any choice He wants * Even if true, freedom is untouched – it only blunts Molinism's help with evil * But it fails: free choices are made **for reasons**, which do not flip with irrelevant changes * Only events in P's **past light cone** count – a finite set * Tiny changes cascade (chaos, quantum) – and God orders **whole histories**, not one choice --- ## Why Not All Saved? ![](pics/AlternateChoices.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem") * Molina: God gives **sufficient grace** to every person and truly wants all saved * Option 1 (**congruism**): for each person some grace would win him – but perhaps no **feasible** world combines them all * Option 2: God could create a world where all freely believe, yet prefers one where some reject Him – as close to Calvinism as possible, **with** freedom * Either way: individuals are predestined, and freedom stays intact --- ## Verdict ![](pics/GodAllPossible.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem") * **Calvinism**: strong sovereignty, but freedom is emptied and God becomes the author of sin * **Arminianism**: real freedom, but sovereignty and planning become hollow * **Molinism**: **full sovereignty** – God plans every detail – **and genuine freedom** – no one is determined * Both streams of Scripture are affirmed at face value, with no appeal to mystery * Molinism is the most compelling of the three – and it does not stand or fall with TULIP: it gives a **free**, individual election --- # Molinism at Work in the Series --- ## Middle Knowledge Already at Work ![](pics/GodLookingIn.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem") * **Inspiration**: God knew what Paul or Luke would freely write in given circumstances – every word God's and theirs ([Inspiration](../RevelationAndFoundations/BibleInspiration.slides.html#slide24)) * **Canon**: middle knowledge guided which books the church would recognize ([Canonicity](../RevelationAndFoundations/Canonicity.slides.html#slide17)) * **The unevangelized**: those who never hear would not have believed if they had heard ([Christ Deck 9](../Christ/Christ.Slides.9.AccessWhoCanBeSavedThroughChrist.html#slide33)) * **Perseverance**: God knows which warnings keep the elect free and faithful (Topic 12.4) --- # Practical Takeaways --- ## All Agree: Whoever Believes Is Saved ![](pics/OpenBible.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem")

"For God so loved the world that He gave His only begotten Son, that **whoever believes in Him** should not perish but have everlasting life."

John 3:16 (NKJV)

"For '**whoever calls** on the name of the LORD shall be saved.'"

Romans 10:13 (NKJV)

* Calvinist, Arminian, Molinist: salvation is by grace through faith in Christ – the gospel is the same --- ## Living It ![](pics/mind.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem") * **Trust**: nothing – not even evil men's choices – escapes God's plan (Genesis 50:20) * **Evangelize**: your witness may be the very circumstance God planned for a friend * **Pray**: prayers are not showpieces; God foreknew them and wove them in * **Humility**: love brothers who see it differently – Bibles are not weapons ("Molinism saves marriages") * **Worship**: only an omniscient mind could steer a world of free creatures to His ends --- ## Credits ![](pics/TulipsAndRoses.jpg style="float: right; width: 9rem") * **Ingmar Bitter**, slides content; *Calvinism, Arminianism, Molinism* deck; TULIP notes * **William Lane Craig**, *Defenders 4: Doctrine of Creation*, Parts 9–12, and *Doctrine of Salvation*, Parts 1–5, 18–21, primary theological source * Full notes: https://www.reasonablefaith.org/podcasts/defenders-podcast-series-4/doctrine-of-creation * **D.
