The four assumptions: * The starting amount of daughter is known * All the daughter came from decay * The rock stayed a closed system * The decay rate never changed --- ## Rocks of Known Age ![](pics/DateOMatic.png style="float: right; width: 10rem") * Test the method where we **know** the answer (potassium–argon dates): * Sunset Crater, Arizona: 200,000+ years – erupted about AD 1065 * Mt. Ngauruhoe, New Zealand: 275,000 years – flows of 1949–1975 * Hualalai basalt, Hawaii: 1.4–22 million years – erupted AD 1801 * Mt. Etna, Sicily: 140,000–350,000 years – erupted 1972 * The usual answer: "excess argon" – but then how do we know it is absent where the age is unknown? --- ## Mount St. Helens ![](pics/MountStHelensDome.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem") * The new lava dome solidified in **1986** * Steve Austin had the dacite dated by potassium–argon: whole rock **350,000 years**; its minerals up to **2.8 million years** * The rock was 10 years old * Argon did not all escape when the lava cooled – assumption 1 failed * "If the dates are never right when we know the age of the rocks, how can we be sure they are correct when we don't know?" --- ## Discordant Dates ![](pics/GrandCanyonBlock.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem") * Grand Canyon, Uinkaret Plateau basalts (the **top** layer): * Potassium–argon: 0.01–17 million years * Rubidium–strontium: 1,270–1,390 million years * Lead–lead isochron: 2,600 million years * The Cardenas Basalt at the canyon **bottom** dates younger than the top lava by rubidium–strontium (about 1,100 million years) * "When the same rock is dated by more than one method, it will often yield different 'ages.'" – John Morris * The published dates are the ones that fit the expected age --- ## Wood in Basalt ![](pics/TreeRings.png style="float: right; width: 10rem") * 1993, Australia: wood buried in a basalt flow, 69 feet deep * The wood, by carbon-14: about **44,000 years** – yet C-14 should be gone * The basalt around it, by potassium–argon: about **45 million years** * Wood and lava must be the **same age** – the lava buried the tree * Two "reliable" methods, the same event, a thousand-fold disagreement --- ## Helium in Zircons ![](pics/RateGroup.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem") * Uranium decaying to lead releases **helium** – a tiny atom that leaks quickly out of crystals * Zircon crystals in granite "1.5 billion years" old still hold **over 58%** of their helium * The measured leak rate fits an age of about **6,000 years**, not billions * So a great deal of decay happened, but **recently and fast** – the RATE team concludes decay rates were once accelerated * RATE: eight years of research by creation physicists and geologists (ICR, CRS) --- # Carbon-14 --- ## How the Carbon Clock Works ![](pics/Carbon14Cycle.png style="float: right; width: 8rem") * Cosmic rays turn nitrogen into radioactive **carbon-14** in the air * Plants take it in; animals eat plants; at death the intake stops * C-14 decays with a half-life of **5,730 years** * Only for once-living things, and only for thousands of years – it can never give millions * Like a burning candle: you must know how tall it was when it was lit – the **starting ratio** of C-14 to C-12 --- ## Is the Atmosphere at Equilibrium? ![](pics/Carbon14Flood.png style="float: right; width: 10rem") * The method assumes C-14 is made as fast as it decays, so the ratio never changes * Willard Libby, its inventor, measured production about **25% faster** than decay – and put it down to experimental error; later work confirmed it * The build-up to equilibrium takes about 30,000 years – so the atmosphere is **still filling up** * A stronger magnetic field in the past shielded out more cosmic rays: less C-14 * The Flood buried about **100 times** today's biosphere carbon as coal and oil – pre-Flood C-12 was far higher, so dates come out **too old** --- ## Carbon-14 Where It Should Not Be ![](pics/Carbon14Ratio.png style="float: right; width: 11rem") * After about 100,000 years no detectable C-14 should remain * Yet **coal** ("300 million years") contains C-14 * So do **diamonds** ("1–3 billion years"), sealed off from contamination * So does fossil wood "200 million years" old * Present in all of them, at levels that fit **thousands**, not millions of years – as expected if all were buried in the Flood --- # Fossils --- ## Buried Fast ![](pics/IchthyosaurBirth.png style="float: right; width: 11rem") * Fossils do not form by slow burial – dead animals are eaten, rot, or scatter within weeks * An ichthyosaur fossilized **while giving birth** * Fish caught mid-swallow; jellyfish with soft outlines preserved * Fossilized butter, a toy car enclosed in rock: fossilization can be quick * The Flood talk's summary: fossils need **rapid burial** – the signature of catastrophe --- ## Polystrate Fossils ![](pics/PolystrateTree.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem") * Upright tree trunks cut through **several layers** – even through coal seams (here in Tennessee) * If each layer took thousands of years, the top of the tree would have rotted before it was buried * So all the layers it crosses formed within **years at most** * One tree, one burial, many "ages" --- ## Soft Tissue in Dinosaur Bone ![](pics/SchweitzerTRex.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem") * Mary Schweitzer: a *T. rex* bone with **stretchy blood vessels** and cell-like structures * Since found in more dinosaurs: collagen, proteins, even DNA-like material * Measured DNA decay: even frozen, it cannot last beyond about 6.8 million years * The bones are "68 million years" old * The rival answers (iron preservation) stretch lab results over tens of millions of years – thousands fit the data far better --- ## The Order of the Fossil Record ![](pics/FossilFish.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem") * About **95%** of fossils are shallow sea creatures – corals, shellfish * Vertebrates are only about **0.25%** – dinosaurs and mammals are a tiny sliver * A global Flood buries the **ocean floor first**, then shore dwellers, then land animals fleeing to higher ground * Moving water **sorts** by size, shape, and density * So a general order is expected – but with **exceptions**, since it was a catastrophe --- ## Exceptions That Break the Order ![](pics/Roraima.jpg style="float: right;
