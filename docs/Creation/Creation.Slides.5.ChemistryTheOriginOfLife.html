width: 7rem") * William Dembski: under this rule you could not infer design even if every atom carried the label "**Made by God**" * Craig: if God really did act to create life, it would be a tragedy – scientifically and personally – to be barred from discovering it * Method should **help** us find the truth about reality, not hinder us * And theologians are under no such rule: we weigh **both** the data of science and the data of revelation --- ## Two Camps ![](pics/TwoViews.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem") * **Necessitism:** the laws of physics and chemistry make life **inevitable** – it should arise often and everywhere * **Contingentism:** life needs many independent lucky factors – a **fantastically improbable** event * Necessitism was popular in the 1960s–70s, but no life on Mars and no lab synthesis after Miller–Urey brought disillusionment * Today most researchers are contingentists * Each camp accuses the other of being **closet creationists** – necessitists say the contingentists' odds amount to a miracle --- ## Fleeing to Outer Space ![](pics/Cosmos.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem") * Edward Steele and 32 colleagues: life came to Earth on comets – the first cell needs "an information hurdle of **superastronomical** proportions" that could not be overcome on Earth "except, we believe, as a **miracle**" * Eugene Koonin: a coupled replication–translation system is "effectively, impossible" – so he appeals to an **infinite multiverse** * But in a multiverse anything happens by chance – even evolution becomes unnecessary (Koonin: "almost nightmarish") * These are sober scientists – driven to such lengths by the odds --- # Step 1: The Building Blocks --- ## Miller–Urey: The Experiment ![](pics/Lightning.jpg style="float: right; width: 11rem") * 1953: Stanley Miller and Harold Urey filled a sealed glass apparatus with **methane, ammonia, hydrogen**, and water vapor * 60,000-volt sparks simulated lightning; a cold trap collected the products * After a week: mostly **tar** – and traces of the simplest amino acids (glycine 1.05%, alanine 0.75%) * Headlines: "the first step toward creating life in a test tube" * Still the most-cited (often the only) evidence for abiogenesis in textbooks --- ## What It Did Not Show (1): The Atmosphere ![](pics/LitmusTest.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem") * Geochemists now think the early atmosphere was **not** methane and ammonia but volcanic – mostly carbon dioxide and nitrogen * *Science:* "the early atmosphere looked nothing like the Miller–Urey situation" * Miller himself later showed he could **not** make organics in an oxidizing atmosphere * Old rocks carry oxidized iron – but oxygen destroys the building blocks, while without oxygen there is no ozone shield against UV * No geological trace of a primordial soup has been found --- ## What It Did Not Show (2): Rigged for Success ![](pics/rube-goldberg-machine.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem") * The **cold trap** pulled products away from the spark before it destroyed them – nature has no such trap * Miller used **chosen** wavelengths of UV and screened out the destructive ones * He removed contaminants and kept sugars out, which react with amino acids * The products were **racemic** – half left-handed, half right-handed * Less than half of life's 20 amino acids, even after hundreds of variations * Bergman: the experiment argues **against** abiogenesis – it shows how much intelligent control is needed --- ## Dead in the Water ![](pics/Dominos.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem") * Building blocks must be linked into chains – proteins average about 330 amino acids * National Academy of Sciences: "Two amino acids do not spontaneously join in water. Rather, the **opposite** reaction is thermodynamically favored" * Undirected energy **destroys** faster than it builds – UV destroys amino acids 10,000 times faster or more than it makes them * Dilution: the oceans could never reach the concentrations required * Thaxton, Bradley, and Olson: no way to **harness** raw energy or **preserve** step-1 products for step 2 --- ## Homochirality: Left and Right Hands ![](pics/MickyMinnie.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem") * Many molecules of life come in two **mirror-image** forms, like left and right hands * Life uses only **left-handed** amino acids and **right-handed** sugars – 100% pure * A single wrong-handed unit disrupts a protein's helix; DNA could not form a stable helix with even one * But ordinary chemistry always yields a **50/50 mix**: "Optically inactive reagents yield optically inactive products" * That is thermodynamics: both forms have the same free energy --- ## Why Chirality Defeats Chance ![](pics/PasswordLock.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem") * Chance of one small homochiral protein of 100 units: 2⁻¹⁰⁰ ≈ **10⁻³⁰** – before even getting the sequence right * Proposed rescues fail: polarized UV light gives 20% purity only after destroying 99% of the material * Quartz, clays, magnetic fields, the weak force: tiny excesses at best * Chemists separate the two forms only by using a pure substance **from a living thing** – life already needed * And pure molecules drift back to 50/50 over time: undirected chemistry heads toward death, not life --- # Step 2: The First Cell --- ## The RNA World ![](pics/proteins.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem") * **Steelman:** in cells, DNA stores information and proteins do the work – a chicken-and-egg problem * But **RNA** can do a bit of both: carry information and catalyze some reactions * So perhaps the first life was RNA alone, which later "invented" DNA and proteins * It is the most prominent origin-of-life hypothesis today * Biochemist Harold Bernhardt titled his 2012 paper "The RNA world hypothesis: the worst theory of the early evolution of life (except for all the others)" --- ## Why the RNA World Fails ![](pics/ComplexWeb.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem") * RNA does not assemble without a skilled chemist guiding each step – Robert Shapiro: "The flaw is in the logic" * RNA is **unstable** – even more so than DNA * RNA catalysis is rare and limited – no evidence it can do all that proteins do * A self-copying RNA needs about 250 nucleotides in the right order: odds about **1 in 10¹⁵⁰** * Koonin: "no one has achieved bona fide self-replication of RNA" --- ## The Chicken and the DVD ![](pics/Polymerase.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem") * A DVD is useless without a DVD player – but what if the only instructions for building the player were **on the DVD**? * In cells, DNA is the disk; the machines that read it and build proteins are the player * And those reading machines are themselves **encoded in the DNA** they read * Salisbury: "everything must happen at once: the entire system must come into being as one unit, or it is worthless" * George Whitesides (Priestley Medal, 2007): life arose spontaneously – "How? **I have no idea**" --- ## The Minimal Cell ![](pics/Science.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem") * Simplest known cell (Mycoplasma genitalium): 482 genes – and it is a parasite living off other cells; a computed bare minimum is 387 protein genes + 43 RNA genes, probably too weak to survive * It needs a membrane, an ATP motor, and DNA copying, **repair**, and reading machines all at once * DNA is highly reactive – about a million bases are damaged in a human cell each day; without repair, information decays * Cell biologist Franklin Harold: how cells came to exist "is, for all practical purposes, **unknown**" * Craig: neither step – building blocks nor assembly – is "anywhere close to being solved" --- ## Not Enough Tries ![](pics/ChanceLawDesign.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem") * Sarfati: 387 enzymes with ~10 fixed positions each → about **1E-5035** – like guessing a 5,000-digit PIN * Minimal cell ≈ 500 proteins of ≈ 330 amino acids: about **1E214500** combinations * Even granting 4 billion years: 1 km of Earth's surface packed with cell-sized trials, one per nanosecond, gives at most **1E71** tries * Odds of success: about **1E-214429** – a PIN with over 200,000 digits * Chance is not an option; and more time does not help – the products just decay --- # Information --- ## DNA Is a Code ![](pics/ATCG-list.jpg style="float: right; width: 11rem") * DNA stores a **four-letter digital code**; three letters (a codon) spell one amino acid, plus start and stop signals * Crick's "sequence hypothesis": the bases work like letters in a written language * Dawkins: "the machine code of the genes is uncannily computer-like" * Bill Gates: DNA is like a program, "far, far more advanced than any software we've ever created" * A human cell's DNA holds about 1,000 books of information --- ## Chance, Necessity, or Mind? ![](pics/GlowWriting.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem") * **Chance:** far too much information (see the numbers above) * **Necessity:** the bases bond to the backbone equally – no chemical law prefers one sequence * Meyer: like a newspaper headline arising from the attraction of ink to paper * **Self-organization:** yields order (ABCABC…) or randomness – not **specified complexity** ("In the beginning God created…") * **Mind:** in all our experience, information comes from intelligence – the same reasoning used by archaeologists and SETI --- ## Natural Selection Cannot Start ![](pics/ThinkingMan.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem") * Natural selection needs something that already **reproduces** – so it cannot explain the first reproducer * Theodosius Dobzhansky (1963): "Prebiological natural selection is a contradiction of terms" * Antony Flew: Darwin's argument "began with a being which already possessed reproductive powers" * Flew, a lifelong atheist, turned to theism in 2004, citing the DNA evidence: "follow the evidence wherever it leads" --- ## Life Requires Information ![](pics/ScrollWithGoldLetters.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem") * Paul Davies: the cell is best described "not in terms of material stuff – hardware – but as information, or software" * "How did stupid atoms spontaneously write their own software? … **Nobody knows**" * "There is no known law of physics able to create information from nothing" * Hubert Yockey: a chance origin of life "which can be accepted on the basis of fact and not faith has not yet been written" * Information points to a **Programmer** --- # The Verdict --- ## Three Theistic Options ![](pics/EvidenceScales.jpg style="float: right;
