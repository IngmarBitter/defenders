# The Invisible Creation: Angels and Demons --- ## Outline * [The Nature of Angels](#slide3) * [The Work of Angels](#slide9) * [Satan: His Names](#slide14) * [Satan: His Origin](#slide19) * [The Nature of Demons](#slide30) * [The Work of Demons](#slide34) * [Practical Takeaways](#slide37) --- ## Where We Are: The Invisible Creation ![](pics/GodWithEarthAndAngles.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem") * Deck 1: Christ created all things "**visible and invisible**, whether thrones or dominions or principalities or powers" (Colossians 1:16) * Decks 2–7 looked at the visible creation; Decks 8–9 at how God governs it * Now the last part: a **created order beyond the physical universe** – angels, Satan, and demons * Not part of the universe, yet still part of creation – they depend on God for their existence --- # The Nature of Angels --- ## Messengers, Not Myths ![](pics/AngelOfGodStatue.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem") * Hebrew *mal'akh* and Greek *angelos* both mean **messenger** – angels are God's messengers to humanity * A higher order of **spiritual beings** who dwell in God's presence and serve His purposes * **Incorporeal**: minds without bodies * Not all angels serve God: there is "the devil and his angels" (Matthew 25:41) * Critics call Satan a legend or a "vehicle of moral teaching" – but Jesus and the apostles treat him as a **real person** (Thompson) --- ## Why Are There Angels? ![](pics/Adoration.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

"Are they not all **ministering spirits** sent forth to minister for those who will inherit salvation?"

Hebrews 1:14 (NKJV)

* To **serve** God for the sake of His people's salvation * To **manifest** God – not as needed mediators: then who mediates between God and the mediator? (the "third man" regress) * To **glorify** God: "Holy, holy, holy is the LORD of hosts" (Isaiah 6:3) --- ## Created, Countless, Ranked ![](pics/GodSpheresOfPower.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem") * **Created**: not eternal, not necessary – "thrones or dominions or principalities or powers" were all made through Christ (Colossians 1:16) * **Innumerable**: "ten thousand times ten thousand" (Daniel 7:10); "an innumerable company of angels" (Hebrews 12:22) * **Ranked**: principalities and powers; Michael is "one of the chief princes" (Daniel 10:13) and "the archangel" (Jude 9) * **Wise**: "the wisdom of the angel of God" (2 Samuel 14:20) --- ## Mighty and Invisible ![](pics/BibleGuarded.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

"So he answered, 'Do not fear, for **those who are with us are more than those who are with them**.' And Elisha prayed, and said, 'LORD, I pray, open his eyes that he may see.' Then the LORD opened the eyes of the young man, and he saw. And behold, the mountain was full of horses and chariots of fire all around Elisha."

2 Kings 6:16–17 (NKJV)

* One angel struck 185,000 Assyrians in a night (2 Kings 19:35) * Unseen because they have no bodies – we do not know who guards us right now --- ## Beyond Space, Able to Appear ![](pics/DreamVision.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem") * **Not bound by physical limits**: an angel appears in Peter's locked cell, frees him, and vanishes (Acts 12:7–10) * **Can assume human form**: Manoah and his wife took the angel for an ordinary man (Judges 13) * "Some have unwittingly entertained angels" (Hebrews 13:2) * At other times they appear with wings or other forms (Revelation) * Assuming a form, they can act in the physical world – move objects, open prison doors --- # The Work of Angels --- ## Angels and the Nations ![](pics/AncientKing.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem")

"But **the prince of the kingdom of Persia** withstood me twenty-one days; and behold, Michael, one of the chief princes, came to help me, for I had been left alone there with the kings of Persia."

Daniel 10:13 (NKJV)

* Three spiritual "princes": of Persia, of Greece (10:20), and Michael, the prince of Israel (10:21, 12:1) * The princes of pagan nations are presumably **fallen angels** – they fight God's angels * An answer to prayer delayed three weeks by unseen battle --- ## Ministering to God's People ![](pics/MeditateAngel.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

"For He shall give His angels charge over you, To keep you in all your ways."

Psalm 91:11 (NKJV)

* An angel fed Elijah under the broom tree (1 Kings 19:5–8) * Angels ministered to Jesus after the temptation (Matthew 4:11) and strengthened Him in Gethsemane (Luke 22:43) * Hebrews 1:14 is about **us**, the church --- ## Justice and Christ's Return ![](pics/GodJudge.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem") * **Execute God's justice**: the Assyrian camp (2 Kings 19:35); Herod "did not give glory to God" and an angel struck him (Acts 12:23) * The seven bowls of wrath are poured out by angels (Revelation 16:1) * **Accompany Christ** at His return "with His mighty angels" (2 Thessalonians 1:7) * **Gather the elect** "from the four winds" (Matthew 24:31), with "the voice of an archangel" (1 Thessalonians 4:16) --- ## Michael and Gabriel ![](pics/HeavenCity.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem") * The only two angels **named** in Scripture – each in Daniel and again in the New Testament * **Michael – the warrior**: chief prince, prince of Israel (Daniel 10:13, 21; 12:1); the archangel who contended with the devil (Jude 9); leads the war in heaven against the dragon (Revelation 12:7) * **Gabriel – the messenger**: explains Daniel's visions (Daniel 8:16; 9:21); "I am Gabriel, who stands in the presence of God" (Luke 1:19); announces John and Jesus (Luke 1:26) --- # Satan: His Names --- ## Satan, the Adversary ![](pics/JesusTemptation.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

"Now there was a day when the sons of God came to present themselves before the LORD, and **Satan also came among them**."

Job 1:6 (NKJV)

* *Satan* (Hebrew, Greek *satanas*): **adversary** – with the article, "**the** adversary" (Job 1–2; Zechariah 3:1–2) * *Diabolos*: the **devil** – the slanderer (1 Peter 5:8) * Among the "sons of God" in heaven – an **angelic being** set against God --- ## A Liar and a Ruler ![](pics/Pinochio.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem")

"You are of your father the devil, and the desires of your father you want to do. He was **a murderer from the beginning**, and does not stand in the truth, because there is no truth in him. When he speaks a lie, he speaks from his own resources, for he is **a liar and the father of it**."

John 8:44 (NKJV)

* "The ruler of this world" (John 14:30); "the whole world lies under the sway of the wicked one" (1 John 5:19) – we live **behind enemy lines** --- ## More Names, One Enemy ![](pics/EvilMoralAndNatural.jpg style="float: right; width: 11rem") * **Beelzebub** – from Baal-Zebub, "lord of the flies", the "ruler of the demons" (Matthew 12:24) * **Prince of the power of the air** (Ephesians 2:2) * **God of this age**, who blinds minds (2 Corinthians 4:4) * **The tempter** (1 Thessalonians 3:5); **the accuser** of our brethren (Revelation 12:10) * **The dragon, that serpent of old** (Revelation 12:9, 20:2); **the evil one** (Matthew 6:13) * Disguised as **an angel of light** (2 Corinthians 11:14) * "**Lucifer**" – only in Isaiah 14:12, and whether that means Satan is debated (next section) --- ## Not an Anti-God ![](pics/GodHoly.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem") * Scripture teaches **no dualism** – no God and anti-God, equal and opposed, like light and darkness * Satan is **not deity**: not eternal, not omnipotent (he must "ask" for Peter, Luke 22:31), not omnipresent, not omniscient * So he is a **creature** – made through Christ with all "principalities or powers" (Colossians 1:16, Deck 1) * Idols are no rival gods either: "the things which the Gentiles sacrifice they sacrifice to demons" (1 Corinthians 10:20) --- # Satan: His Origin --- ## Created Good ![](pics/GodCreation.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

"Then God saw **everything** that He had made, and indeed it was **very good**. So the evening and the morning were the sixth day."

Genesis 1:31 (NKJV)

* God did not **create** Satan evil – Satan **became** evil * But Scripture never explains his origin directly: in Genesis 3 the serpent simply appears * So we piece together the clues – starting with two famous and disputed texts --- ## Isaiah 14: The Fallen Day Star ![](pics/UpOrDown.jpg style="float: right; width: 11rem")

"How you are fallen from heaven, O **Lucifer**, son of the morning! How you are cut down to the ground, You who weakened the nations! For you have said in your heart: '**I will ascend into heaven**, I will exalt my throne above the stars of God [...] **I will be like the Most High**.'"

Isaiah 14:12–14 (NKJV)

* The frame: "this proverb against **the king of Babylon**" (14:4) * "Lucifer" (Latin, "light-bearer") renders the Hebrew for "shining one" – the morning star --- ## Ezekiel 28: The Cherub in Eden ![](pics/EveReachingForFruit.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem")

"[...] Take up a lamentation for **the king of Tyre** [...] You were the seal of perfection, Full of wisdom and perfect in beauty. **You were in Eden, the garden of God** [...] You were **the anointed cherub** who covers [...] You were perfect in your ways from the day you were created, Till iniquity was found in you."

Ezekiel 28:12–15 (NKJV)

* The frame: "you are **a man, and not a god**" (28:2); "by the abundance of your **trading** [...] you sinned" (28:16) --- ## The Case for Satan Behind the King ![](pics/Lightning.jpg style="float: right; width: 11rem") * The traditional reading, held by many evangelicals * The language **exceeds any human king**: a throne above the stars, "like the Most High"; in Eden, an anointed cherub, perfect "from the day you were created" * The prophet **looks through** the king to the spiritual power behind him – as Daniel 10 shows real "princes" behind Persia and Greece * It fits the rest of Scripture: pride as the devil's sin (1 Timothy 3:6), a fall from heaven (Luke 10:18), the Eden serpent (Revelation 12:9) --- ## The Case for the Earthly King ![](pics/AncientKing.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem") * Craig's reading: both texts are addressed to **named earthly kings**, Babylon and Tyre * Isaiah 14 continues: "Is this **the man** who made the earth tremble?" (14:16) * Ezekiel 28 condemns **trade** and violence – not applicable to an angel * The high language is **hyperbole** for royal pride – the king who claims "I am a god" * Critics say the prophets borrowed a myth of an angelic fall – but no such myth is known: shapes in the clouds * So an angelic fall may be **true**, but reading it into these texts is eisegesis --- ## Weighing the Two Readings ![](pics/EvidenceScales.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem") * Both sides agree the text is **addressed to a king**; they differ on whether it **also** points past him * Both sides agree Satan **fell through pride** – the question is only which texts teach it * So the doctrine should **not rest** on Isaiah 14 and Ezekiel 28 alone * A careful position: the texts are about the kings first; the Satan reading is possible and ancient, but **not certain** * The clear texts come next – and they settle the doctrine by themselves --- ## The Angels Who Sinned ![](pics/Lightning.jpg style="float: right; width: 11rem")

"For if God did not spare **the angels who sinned**, but cast them down to hell and delivered them into chains of darkness, to be reserved for judgment;"

2 Peter 2:4 (NKJV)

"And the angels who **did not keep their proper domain**, but left their own abode, He has reserved in everlasting chains under darkness for the judgment of the great day;"

Jude 6 (NKJV)

* Some are chained; others, with Satan, still roam – within limits --- ## Pride Before the Fall ![](pics/HolyPagan.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem")

"not a novice, lest being **puffed up with pride** he fall into the same condemnation as the devil."

1 Timothy 3:6 (NKJV)

* "The devil has **sinned from the beginning**" (1 John 3:8); "I saw Satan fall like lightning from heaven" (Luke 10:18) * "Elect angels" (1 Timothy 5:21) implies others who are not * So: angels created good, with **free will**; some, led by Satan, rebelled in pride --- ## When Did Satan Fall? ![](pics/GodCosmosClock.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem") * Angels were present and **rejoicing** when the Earth's foundations were laid (Job 38:4–7) – created early, likely on day 1 * Many say they also fell **before** the Earth was made (Thompson) * But at the end of day 6 everything was still "**very good**" (Genesis 1:31) * So the fall came **after day 6 and before Genesis 3** – any time before Satan tempted Eve * This rules out the gap theory's pre-Adamic fall of Satan and ruined world (Deck 2) --- ## No Redemption for Demons ![](pics/DivineJustice.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem")

"Then He will also say to those on the left hand, 'Depart from Me, you cursed, into the **everlasting fire prepared for the devil and his angels**:"

Matthew 25:41 (NKJV)

* Origen hoped even Satan would be saved – the church rejected it * Christ took on **flesh and blood**, not angelic nature (Hebrews 2:14) – His atonement is for humans * The elect angels are sealed in their choice; God may know the fallen would never repent * Hell was **not prepared for people** – no human needs to go there --- # The Nature of Demons --- ## Personal, Spirit, and Evil ![](pics/SuppressTruth.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem")

"So the great dragon was cast out, that serpent of old, called the Devil and Satan, **who deceives the whole world**; he was cast to the earth, and his angels were cast out with him."

Revelation 12:9 (NKJV)

* **Intelligent**: the spirit of divination (Acts 16:16–18); Satan's cunning (2 Corinthians 11:3) * **Spirits**: "demons" and "spirits" used interchangeably (Matthew 8:16) * **Malevolent**: "unclean spirits" (Mark 1:27) --- ## Organized Against Us ![](pics/PrayInStorm.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem")

"For we do not wrestle against flesh and blood, but against **principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this age**, against spiritual hosts of wickedness in the heavenly places."

Ephesians 6:12 (NKJV)

* Like the angels, demons form **ranks of authority** * Even Michael did not revile the devil but said "The Lord rebuke you!" (Jude 8–10; 2 Peter 2:10–11) --- ## Subject to Jesus' Name ![](pics/RansomTheory.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem")

"And He said to them, 'I saw Satan fall like lightning from heaven. Behold, I give you the authority to trample on serpents and scorpions, and **over all the power of the enemy**, and nothing shall by any means hurt you. Nevertheless do not rejoice in this, that the spirits are subject to you, but rather **rejoice because your names are written in heaven**.'"

Luke 10:18–20 (NKJV)

* Superhuman strength (Mark 5:1–4), yet "Legion" begs Jesus (Mark 5:9) * The name is **authority, not magic**: the sons of Sceva were beaten (Acts 19:13–16) --- # The Work of Demons --- ## Blinding and Snatching ![](pics/TwoViews.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

"whose minds **the god of this age has blinded**, who do not believe, lest the light of the gospel of the glory of Christ, who is the image of God, should shine on them."

2 Corinthians 4:4 (NKJV)

* Unbelievers are in "the snare of the devil" (2 Timothy 2:26) – so correct them "in humility" * Satan takes away the word that was sown (Mark 4:15) * Demons know their end: "Have You come here to torment us before the time?" (Matthew 8:29) --- ## Destroy, Possess, Harass, Accuse ![](pics/DreamVision.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem")

"Be sober, be vigilant; because your adversary the devil walks about like a roaring lion, **seeking whom he may devour**."

1 Peter 5:8 (NKJV)

* **Destroy** believers: the tempter (1 Thessalonians 3:5); his devices (2 Corinthians 2:11) * **Possess**: the Gadarene demoniac; "Satan entered him" – Judas (John 13:27) * **Harass**: "Satan hindered us" (1 Thessalonians 2:18); Paul's thorn (2 Corinthians 12:7) * **Accuse** believers "day and night" (Revelation 12:10) --- # Practical Takeaways --- ## Submit, Then Resist ![](pics/StrengthThroughChrist.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

"Therefore **submit to God**. **Resist the devil** and he will flee from you."

James 4:7 (NKJV)

* First submit – we cannot resist in our own strength, only in the Spirit's * Jesus resisted every temptation with **the word of God** (Matthew 4:1–11) * "Watch and pray, lest you enter into temptation" (Matthew 26:41) --- ## Put On the Whole Armor ![](pics/Prayer.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem") * Against the powers of Ephesians 6:12: truth, righteousness, the gospel of peace, faith, salvation, and the sword of the Spirit – **the word of God** (Ephesians 6:11–18) * The battle is won **before** it starts: daily prayer, Bible reading, worship, sharing the gospel, and serving in a local church * We cannot "do what Jesus would do" without the character of Jesus (Dallas Willard) --- ## The Victory Is Already Won ![](pics/DiedForOurSins.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

"Having **disarmed principalities and powers**, He made a public spectacle of them, triumphing over them in it."

Colossians 2:15 (NKJV)

"You are of God, little children, and have overcome them, because **He who is in you is greater than he who is in the world**."

1 John 4:4 (NKJV)

* Christ came "to destroy the works of the devil" (1 John 3:8) --- ## Neither Fear nor Fascination ![](pics/MeditateAngel.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem") * **No fear**: Satan is a creature on a leash; the mountain is full of chariots of fire (2 Kings 6:17) * **No fascination**: do not rejoice that spirits obey – rejoice that your **names are written in heaven** (Luke 10:20) * **No denial**: a real enemy – be sober and vigilant (1 Peter 5:8) * **No angel worship**: angels are fellow servants who point to God (Colossians 2:18; Revelation 22:8–9) * Not every trouble is a demon – but none is outside God's providence (Deck 8) --- ## The Doctrine of Creation in One Slide ![](pics/Cosmos.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem") * **1** God created everything **out of nothing** and keeps it in being * **2** Genesis 1–11 is a **diamond**: six real days at its core, every true facet kept * **3** Physics: the universe **began** and is **fine-tuned** – starlight is an open question, not a defeater * **4** Geology: the rocks read as **one great catastrophe** – the Flood, not millions of years * **5–7** Life, living kinds, and **man in God's image** need a Creator – not chance * **8–9** God **governs** all He made without cancelling our freedom, and is free to act in it by miracle * **10** The **invisible creation** too is His – and Christ has triumphed over its rebels --- ## Credits ![](pics/GodWithEarthAndAngles.jpg style="float: right;
