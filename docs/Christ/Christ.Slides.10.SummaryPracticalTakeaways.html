# Doctrine of Christ Summary and Practical Takeaways --- ## Outline * [The Person of Christ](#slide3) * [The Death of Christ](#slide8) * [The Resurrection](#slide12) * [Access to Salvation](#slide16) * [The Whole in One View](#slide20) * [Practical Takeaways](#slide23) --- ## The Journey So Far ![](pics/ChristOverview.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem") * We set out to answer two questions: **who is Jesus?** (His person) and **what did He do?** (His work) * His work unfolded in three movements: His **death**, His **resurrection**, and the **access** to salvation He opens * This deck gathers the whole arc into one view – and asks what it means for how we live * Not a dry doctrine, but the heart of the Christian faith --- # The Person of Christ --- ## Fully God, Fully Man ![](pics/Trinity.and.JesusTrueGodTrueMan.png style="float: right; width: 22rem") * The New Testament affirms both the full **deity** and the full **humanity** of Jesus Christ * He is not a demigod, not God in a human costume, not a man adopted into divinity * One person who is at once truly God and truly man * Every heresy the church rejected erred by shortchanging one side or the other --- ## One Person, Two Natures ![](pics/Trinity.and.JesusTrueGodTrueMan.png style="float: right; width: 22rem") * **Chalcedon (451):** one person in two complete natures – "without confusion, change, division, or separation" * Craig's model: the *Logos* as the archetypal soul of the man Jesus, the divine largely **subconscious** in His earthly life – a genuine human consciousness that grew, was tempted, and prayed * The incarnation is **addition**, not subtraction: the Son assumed humanity without ceasing to be God --- ## The Rejected Extremes ![](pics/FalseModels.Stack.png style="float: right; width: 3.5rem") * Chalcedon's four "withouts" rule out every wrong turn, ancient and modern – each keeps part of the truth by sacrificing the rest: * **Docetism** – Christ only *seemed* human: no real flesh, no real death * **Apollinarianism** – a human body, but the human mind replaced by the *Logos* * **Nestorianism** – two persons side by side, rather than one * **Eutychianism** – the two natures blended into a third thing, not God, not man * **Radical Kenosis** – deity partially set aside: God gives up being God * **Metamorphosis** – divine, then human, then divine again: never both at once * Every one of them shortchanges either the deity or the humanity – or the unity of the person --- ## Why the God-Man? ![](pics/DiedForOurSins.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem") * The person exists for the work: only a **God-man** can be our Savior * **Truly human** – so He can stand in our place and die * **Truly divine** – so His sacrifice carries infinite worth, sufficient for all * Christology is never abstract: who Christ is grounds everything He accomplishes --- # The Death of Christ --- ## A Diamond with Many Facets ![](pics/diamond.png style="float: right; width: 7rem") * The cross is not one theory but a **diamond** with many facets: * **Penal Substitution** – Christ bears in our place the full penalty our sins deserved: physical, spiritual, and eternal death * The sacrifice, the Suffering Servant, our representative at the bar of divine justice (Isaiah 53:5-6) * The **root facet** – the other three are not rivals to it, but depend on it * **Satisfaction** – God's retributive justice is not waived or relaxed but fully honored * Justice demands proportionality: infinite guilt matched with Christ's infinite worth (Rom 3:25f) * Grounded in penal substitution: justice is satisfied **by the penalty actually being borne**, not by God agreeing to accept something less (contra Scotus) --- ## Facets That Rest on the Root ![](pics/diamond.png style="float: right; width: 7rem") * **Ransom / Redemption** – captives are truly set free; Christ defeats sin, death, and the powers that held us * Scripture frames salvation as liberation: bought with a price, a public triumph over principalities (Colossians 2:15) * Grounded in penal substitution: Satan's lever is **accusation**, not ownership – discharge guilt and accusation has no ground: guilt discharged → accuser silenced → captives freed * **Moral Influence** – cross shows God's love and transforms cold hearts (Rom 5:8) * Real – but a death whose only purpose is to show love makes no sense * Grounded in penal substitution: the cross moves us **because** it first accomplished something real – Dale's rescuer runs into the burning house only if a child is truly inside * No single facet is sufficient; together they reveal the full glory of Christ's death --- ## Defended Against Its Critics ![](pics/PenalSubstitution.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem") * **"It's incoherent"** – guilt cannot transfer from the guilty to the innocent * Human criminal law already does it – imputation and vicarious liability; Christ is legally guilty * **"It's unjust"** – punishing an innocent man is wrong * He was not legally innocent once sin was imputed – and He bore it voluntarily * **"Finite suffering cannot pay an eternal penalty"** * Infinite in **value** by the divinity of His person – it need not be eternal in duration * **"Jesus forsaken tears the Trinity apart"** (Matthew 27:46, quoting Psalm 22) * On the common reading Christ points to the psalm, which ends in vindication – no tear * And if the desolation was real, it was borne in His **human nature** – judicial, not a tear in the divine essence --- # The Resurrection --- ## The Earliest Creed ![](pics/ChristOverview.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem")

"For I delivered to you first of all that which I also received: that Christ died for our sins according to the Scriptures, and that He was buried, and that He rose again the third day according to the Scriptures,"

1 Corinthians 15:3-4 (NKJV)

* The biblical evidence: an **early creed** (within five years of the cross), a **witness list** open to cross-examination, the **empty tomb**, and **transformed disciples** * The resurrection body is physical yet glorified – continuity through transformation, not a body-swap * This is testimony that is early, public, checkable, and physically grounded --- ## The Best Explanation ![](pics/ChristOverview.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem") * The crucifixion itself is bedrock – attested by friend and foe alike (Tacitus, Josephus, the Talmud) * The historical apologetic rests on **three facts** nearly all scholars grant: the empty tomb, the post-mortem appearances, and the origin of the disciples' belief * Run through McCullagh's criteria, every rival – conspiracy, swoon, Islam's substitution, wrong tomb, legend, hallucination – fails on scope, power, or plausibility * Nor is it "too improbable": as with a lottery win, the question is which is more improbable – the event, or the evidence arising without it * "**God raised Jesus from the dead**" best explains all three historical facts – especially given natural theology and Jesus' own radical claims --- ## Two Sides of One Coin ![](pics/ChristWork.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem")

"who was delivered up because of our offenses, and was raised because of our justification."

Romans 4:25 (NKJV)

* Christ's **death** saves us – the penalty paid in full * The resurrection's role is to **prove and assure**: it vindicates Jesus' claims and demonstrates God's power to raise us too * Cross and resurrection are two sides of one saving act – the verdict secured, then publicly declared --- # Access to Salvation --- ## Christ Alone ![](pics/NoOtherName.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem")

"Nor is there salvation in any other, for there is no other name under heaven given among men by which we must be saved."

Acts 4:12 (NKJV)

* **Christian particularism:** salvation is through Christ alone – needed by all, offered to all, but not accepted by all * The sting: not that Christianity is *different*, but that it says other worldviews are **wrong** * This is not exclusivism that shuts people out; God loves all and desires all to be saved * The fallacies against it (arrogance, cultural relativism) are self-defeating – the real challenge is the fate of the unevangelized --- ## No One Lost by Accident ![](pics/HandsEarth.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

"And He has made from one blood every nation of men to dwell on all the face of the earth, and has determined their preappointed times and the boundaries of their dwellings, so that they should seek the Lord, in the hope that they might grope for Him and find Him, though He is not far from each one of us;"

Acts 17:26-27 (NKJV)

* Salvation is universally **accessible** through general revelation, though rarely accessed * By His **middle knowledge**, God so orders history that those who would respond to the Gospel hear it – and no one is lost by geographical or historical accident * God is both all-powerful and all-loving, and some are still freely lost – with no contradiction --- ## Then Why Missions? ![](pics/DivineAppointments.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem") * If God already places responders where the Gospel reaches them, is evangelism pointless? * No – God sends the Gospel to people **because** He has placed responders among them: missions **maximizes** the saved * Not guilt but **joy** – **divine appointments** await in your path * Hearing the Gospel is a **bonus gift** – not the only road to God, but by far the easiest * **Christian particularism** is not undermined by religious diversity – it is coherent, biblical, and even plausible * Anyone anywhere who wants – or even *would* want – to be saved, will be --- # The Whole in One View --- ## Four Movements ![](pics/ChristOverview.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem") * **Person:** the God-man, fully God and fully man – the one qualified to save * **Death:** the many-faceted atonement, rooted in penal substitution – the price paid * **Resurrection:** the vindication of His person and work – historically the best explanation of the evidence * **Access:** Christ alone, offered to all, applied by a God who leaves no one lost by accident --- ## Two Ways to Know ![](pics/ChristOverview.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem") * **Existential** – the living encounter with the risen Christ, by which the simplest believer knows He is real * **Evidential** – the historical and philosophical case, which confirms and commends the faith * The pattern throughout: **establish the facts, then infer the best explanation** * Both roads lead to the same Lord – head and heart together --- # Practical Takeaways --- ## Worship ![](pics/GodOutstretchedArms.jpg style="float: right; width: 9rem")

"Therefore God also has highly exalted Him and given Him the name which is above every name, that at the name of Jesus every knee should bow, of those in heaven, and of those on earth, and of those under the earth, and that every tongue should confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father."

Philippians 2:9-11 (NKJV)

* Because of **who He is** and **what He has done**, Jesus is worthy of worship * Christology fuels adoration: doctrine is not the enemy of devotion but its fuel --- ## Assurance ![](pics/GiftOfFaith.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem")

"There is therefore now no condemnation to those who are in Christ Jesus, who do not walk according to the flesh, but according to the Spirit."

Romans 8:1 (NKJV)

* Our standing rests on **Christ's finished work**, not on our feelings or performance * The penalty is paid, the verdict is in, the tomb is empty – the case is closed * Assurance is not presumption; it is resting in what God Himself has secured --- ## Hope ![](pics/HeavenCity.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem") * Christ is the **firstfruits** – His resurrection is the forerunner of ours * The grave is not the last word: we await glorious, immortal, imperishable bodies * This reframes suffering and death: our labor "is not in vain in the Lord" (1 Corinthians 15:58) * We grieve, but not as those without hope --- ## Holiness ![](pics/MoralInfluence.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem") * The cross is not only **pardon** but **transformation** – justification *and* sanctification * United to Christ in His death and resurrection, we are dead to sin and alive to God * His self-giving love, rightly seen, draws the heart and remakes the life (the moral-influence facet, rooted in the objective work) * Grace is free, but it is never cheap --- ## Mission ![](pics/DivineAppointments.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem") * If salvation is through Christ alone, then love **compels** proclamation * Missions is not driven by guilt but by joy: God has gone ahead and prepared hearts – there are **divine appointments** waiting in your path * Compassion for those of other faiths is not pretending they are safe, but bringing them the life-giving Gospel * We go out confident that the risen Lord goes with us --- ## A Closing Word ![](pics/ChristWork.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem") * The Doctrine of Christ is not a museum of settled definitions – it is the living center of the faith * Every thread – incarnation, atonement, resurrection, access – converges on a Person to be known, trusted, and adored * "To know Christ and to make Him known" * May the study of who He is and what He has done deepen both our confidence and our worship --- ## Credits ![](pics/ChristWork.jpg style="float: right;
