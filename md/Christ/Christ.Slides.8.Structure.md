# Deck 8 Structure — The Resurrection: Defeating the Alternatives

Working document for the restructure. Old slide numbers in parentheses for traceability.

## Agreed changes

1. **Reorder:** Three Facts moves before the skeptics.
   - Deck then actually walks the two-step method it announces: method → Step 1 (facts) → Step 2 (explanations and weighing)
2. **Trim the Facts section** — state the facts, refer back to Deck 7 for their defense; keep only what is new and load-bearing:
   - Empty tomb: 5 slides → 2; Appearances: 2 slides → 1
   - Origin of belief: kept at full depth — not in Deck 7, and it powers the myth and hallucination refutations
3. **Combine History of Skepticism + Weighing the Hypotheses into one section**, chronological, statement → rebuttal pairs (the pattern the Islam slides already use). McCullagh's grid comes first; each theory is introduced and immediately weighed.
   - The Quest for the Historical Jesus folds into the Paulus slide it was setting up
   - Grass & Pannenberg and Marxsen & Spong (never weighed separately before) stand as single statement-plus-verdict slides
   - "Why Study the Skeptics?" dropped; its "internet skeptics recycling 200-year-old theories" line moves into the Conspiracy rebuttal
   - Gospel of Peter contrast (old 24) moves into the Myth rebuttal
   - "Translation, Not Resurrection" stays last as capstone against the whole vision family

Net: 60 slides (0–59) → 53 slides (0–52). Outline drops to five sections.
