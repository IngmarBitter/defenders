# Creation Unit – Review Findings and Fixes

Working doc (plain markdown, md-only, never mirrored to `docs/`). Findings of the full unit review of 2026-09-26, each with a proposed fix. Numbered continuously so items can be referenced by number. Obvious errors found in the same review were already fixed and committed; only content work remains here.

Decisions: no slide-count ceiling – take as much space as a proper representation of the ideas needs; a deck may take several sessions. Every science deck (3–7) opens with the motivation slide "Why This Deck: Has Science Disproven the Bible?" (done).

## Deck 2 – The Days of Genesis

1. **Craig's positive case for mytho-history is never stated.** The Facet slide only defines the view. Fix: one new slide before polishing, from Excursus 13 and 17–18: shared etiological motifs with ANE myths, the plasticity of those myths, and societies not taking their myths literally. Then the polishing has something to cut.
2. **Genesis 2:5 harmonization is the one Craig already rebutted.** Craig calls "plants of the field means cultivated plants" too clever by half, since 2:5 gives no rain and no man as reasons and man tills before the Fall. Fix: reword to "2:5 names conditions, not causes: the two Hebrew terms are cultivated grain and wild shrub; the garden is planted for the man in 2:8, a close-up of day 6." Add a bullet on 2:19: "formed" can be pluperfect, and only beasts of the field and birds are brought.
3. **"Fantastic equals miraculous" misses Craig's point** that the fruit worked even against God's will. Fix: replace with three bullets: the fruit's effect is a created property like bread nourishing, so God's command opposed the eating, not the tree; the knowledge of evil came from the act of disobedience, not chemistry; God guards the tree of life because it is real and works, and it returns in Revelation 22:2. Keep "concordism in reverse" as the closer.
4. **Two Craig objections to YEC go unanswered:** day 2's ocean draining in a day against the Flood taking 150 days, and Noah being alive at Abraham's birth. Fix: add both to "More Objections" with answers: fiat-and-fulfilment language is the same day by Craig's own reading in Excursus 5; the lifespan overlap is what Ussher's chart shows and nothing in the text objects, so the objection is incredulity, not exegesis.
5. **Day-Age arithmetic slide is the weakest argument.** 45 years per generation is a chosen number. Fix: replace with the Bible's own numbers: Exodus 12:40 gives 430 years in Egypt, 1 Kings 6:1 gives 480 years from the Exodus to Solomon's temple around 966 BC, so Joseph is in Egypt around 1880 BC. Drop the 6000 BC Nile bullet.
6. **"Many scholars agree the author meant ordinary days" is unsourced.** Fix: use James Barr's 1984 letter, which is in Sources/Genesis, as a hostile witness, and name von Rad and Pannenberg as the view's holders on the Literal Naive slide.
7. **Hosea 6:2 answer is vague.** Fix: state Sarfati's point from the Series 2 notes: the "two, three" idiom of Job 5:19 and Amos 1:3 does not count days, and "quick and sure" only works if the days are short.
8. **No-death claim lacks the nephesh distinction.** Fix: one bullet on "What Everyone Ate": plants are not nephesh chayyah, so eating plants is not death. Source is the CMI no-death article.
9. **Chart order differs from deck order.** The chart is pinned by its science and age axes. Fix: one bullet on "How Each View Is Cut": the chart sorts by science and age; we walk it from the core outward, ending at the closest rival.
10. **Walton polishing uses the Lego bullet where Craig's stronger point exists.** Fix: use Craig's eyewitness question from Excursus 7: Walton admits the world before the seven days lacked only humans in God's image and God's presence.

## Deck 3 – Physics

11. **The deck uses Big Bang evidence for the Kalam, then says "the universe never was that hot."** Fix: retitle "Scientific Problems (Lisle)" to "Problems for the Naturalistic Story" and open with "What we keep: the expansion and the beginning are observed. What we question: the billions of years and stars before earth." Then monopoles and antimatter argue against the hot naturalistic early universe, not against a beginning.
12. **Flatness listed as a Big Bang problem, but it is the fine-tuning argument.** Fix: drop it from that slide, or say "flatness embarrasses the atheist, not Ross; it is fine-tuning."
13. **Redshift and CMB are named as evidence and never answered.** Fix: one bullet on "Answering Big Bang Creation": the redshift and CMB are real; young-creation cosmologies try to explain them and are unfinished; we hold the timeline on the text, not on finished physics.
14. **The ASC critique is unsourced and inverts Lisle's point.** "A convention describes, it does not explain" treats Lisle's own claim as a flaw. Fix: replace both bullets and the summary line with Faulkner's nuance: Lisle does not claim the light physically arrives instantly; Genesis speaks from the earth's surface where the stars' light appeared on Day 4; the open question is whether the Bible really uses cosmic local time.
15. **Dasha "a miracle cannot be tested" is not Faulkner's.** Fix: use Faulkner's "Probably not" and "We don't know", add the real open question of light from events after Day 4 such as SN 1987A, and Lisle's flashlight analogy as the answer to "untestable".
16. **Crab Nebula bullet is a non-argument on the wrong slide.** Fix: cut it, or restate Riddle's actual point on a young-universe slide: all observed supernova remnants look under 10,000 years old.
17. **No positive young-universe astronomy at all.** Fix: one new slide "Clocks That Look Young" from Lisle's conclusion and Riddle: spiral arms winding up, hot blue stars, comets and the unobserved Oort cloud, moon recession, with Lisle's caveat that these too involve assumptions.
18. **Observational versus historical science distinction is missing.** Fix: state it on "The Problem": distances are repeatable observation, ages are historical inference. Add Lisle's flashlight on the hidden-assumptions slide.
19. **Dated "never observed" star-formation quotes** from 1976 to 2002 will draw pushback. Fix: use DeYoung's actual words, "the complete birth has never been watched", and "no confirmed Population III star" instead of "not one".
20. **Sarfati is credited but unused.** Fix: drop him, or use his firing-squad answer to "we should not be surprised" on the fine-tuning slide.

## Deck 4 – Geology

21. **"The Old-Earth Case at Its Strongest" raises reefs, deserts, and soils and never answers them.** Fix: add one slide "Answering the Strongest Case": reefs are mostly transported carbonate debris, desert sandstones show underwater cross-bedding as at Coconino, "soils" lack roots and horizons. Needs a CMI or AiG source added to the folder.
22. **Carbon-14 calibration is unmentioned.** Any science-literate listener will object that radiocarbon is calibrated against tree rings. Fix: one bullet: modern dating calibrates with tree rings and varves, but the calibration itself assumes uniform pre-Flood conditions.
23. **RATE accelerated decay omits the heat problem.** Fix: a sub-bullet "open question: where did the heat go?" and attribute the 6,000-year figure to the RATE team's diffusion measurements.
24. **Local-flood steelman is weak.** "Covered could mean rain ran down" is not Ross's argument. Fix: state his real one, lower regional mountains and "under the whole heaven" as the observer's horizon, then answer with the ark's need, the 150 days, and the rainbow promise.
25. **Strong sourced material is missing:** population growth from eight people at 0.5% per year, the founding dates of Babylon, Egypt, and Greece just after Peleg, erets and Luke 17:27, and "could the animals fit". Fix: two new slides in the Flood section, from FloodTalk and Course.12. The population slide also backs "nations starting 4,400 years ago", which is currently asserted without evidence.
26. **Credits:** add John Morris, quoted on a slide. Optionally footnote Craig as the steelmanned old-earth position.
