# Recap: A Beginning and a Design

---

## The Universe Had a Beginning

![](pics/BigBangModel.png style="float: right; width: 11rem")

* The Kalam argument: whatever begins to exist has a cause; the universe began to exist; so the universe has a cause
* The physical evidence:
  * Expansion traced back ends at a **boundary** – the beginning of space-time
  * Thermodynamics: the universe is running down – so no infinite past
  * Borde–Guth–Vilenkin: any expanding universe has a **finite past**
* The cause: uncaused, timeless, spaceless, immaterial, enormously powerful, personal
* Full case: [Kalam Cosmological Argument](../NaturalTheology/KalamCosmological.slides.html)

---

## Fine-Tuned for Life

![](pics/FineTuningOfTheUniverse.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem")

* Gravity, the strong and weak nuclear forces, and electromagnetism sit in a razor-thin life-permitting range
* Strong force 0.3% stronger: only heavy elements; 2% weaker: only hydrogen
* Cosmological constant: fine-tuned to about 1 part in 10<sup>120</sup>
* Not by **chance** (too improbable), not by **law** (no law fixes the values) – **design** is the best explanation
* Full case: [Fine-Tuning of the Universe](../FineTuning/FineTuning.slides.html)

# The Big Bang

---

## What the Big Bang Model Says

![](pics/Cosmos.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem")

* The universe began about 13.8 billion years ago from an extremely hot, dense state
* Space itself expanded; energy cooled into hydrogen and helium
* Gas clouds collapsed into stars and galaxies; stars forged heavier elements and exploded
* Earth and planets formed later from that debris
* Main evidence: redshift of galaxies, the cosmic microwave background, light-element abundances

---

## What It Gets Right: A Beginning

![](pics/NatureAndBible.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem")

* Before the Big Bang, many scientists held an **eternal**, steady-state universe
* The evidence forced a **beginning** – exactly what Genesis 1:1 said all along
* Physicist Paul Davies: the Big Bang "represents a **true miracle**"
* So the Kalam argument rightly uses this evidence: the universe began to exist
* Keep this: a beginning out of nothing is biblical (Deck 1)

---

## The Opposing View: Big Bang Creation

![](pics/GodUmlimitedPower.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

* **Hugh Ross** (Reasons to Believe), astronomer, fairly stated:
  * The Big Bang was a major factor in his coming to Christ: a beginning implies a **Beginner**
  * Bible authors described its features long before scientists did
  * Space, time, matter, and energy begin together – creation out of nothing (Hebrews 11:3)
  * The fine-tuned expansion rate is evidence of **design**
  * The days are long ages; the Bible and nature tell the **same story** (Deck 2)

---

## Ross: "He Stretches Out the Heavens"

![](pics/GodWordEarth.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

<p style="font-family: 'EB Garamond', serif; font-style: italic;">"It is He who sits above the circle of the earth, And its inhabitants are like grasshoppers, **Who stretches out the heavens like a curtain**, And spreads them out like a tent to dwell in."</p><p style="text-align: right; margin-top: -1.25em; margin-bottom: -0.75em;"> <em>Isaiah 40:22 (NKJV)</em></p>

* Ross reads this (and Job 9:8, Isaiah 42:5; 44:24) as **cosmic expansion**
* Day 4: a hazy atmosphere clears, so sun, moon, and stars become **visible**
* Answer: a poetic picture of God's power – not a cosmology lesson (concordism)

![](pics/RosetteNebula.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem")

<p style="font-family: 'EB Garamond', serif; font-style: italic;">"Then God made two great lights: the greater light to rule the day, and the lesser light to rule the night. **He made the stars also.** [...] So the evening and the morning were the fourth day."</p><p style="text-align: right; margin-top: -1.25em; margin-bottom: -0.75em;"> <em>Genesis 1:16, 19 (NKJV)</em></p>

* "Made" (asah), not "became visible" – the text states when they were made

## Where We Part Ways (3): Natural Process Alone?

![](pics/ArrogantScientist.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem")

* Ross: "The entire process of stellar evolution is by **natural process alone**. We do not have to invoke Divine intervention at any stage"
* Scripture: the heavens were made **by His word** (Psalm 33:6)
* Jason Lisle (AiG): the Big Bang was an attempt to explain origins **without God** – so it cannot simply be "added" to the Bible
* For Ross the stars form by natural process alone; Genesis 1:16 and Psalm 33:6 say God made them

---

## Scientific Problems (Lisle)

![](pics/ScaleWithQuestions.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem")

* **Missing monopoles** – a hot Big Bang should have made many; none found
* **Flatness** – expansion balanced against gravity to an extreme precision
* **Antimatter** – energy makes equal matter and antimatter; we see almost only matter
* **Inflation** fixes several problems – but what started and stopped it?
* 2004 open letter by secular scientists: the model leans on unobserved **inflation, dark matter, and dark energy**

---

## Answering Big Bang Creation

![](pics/TwoViews.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

* Grant the good: a beginning, fine-tuning, a Beginner – we use them too
* But reading the Big Bang **into** Genesis is concordism – the text's order and six days must bend
* Models come and go: if the Big Bang is revised, does the Bible's "meaning" change with it?
* Better: take the **beginning** from the evidence, take the **timeline** from the text
* God's Word does not need updating: He "got it exactly right the first time" (Lisle)

# Distant Starlight

---

## The Problem

![](pics/nebula.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

* Andromeda: about 2 million light-years away; the James Webb telescope sees galaxies over 12 billion
* If light travels at a fixed speed, how can we see them in a universe thousands of years old?
* A light-year is a **distance**, not a time – but the problem is real
* Distance measures are sound observational science – space really is huge (Psalm 19:1)
* Those who hold a young creation must acknowledge it and answer it (Danny Faulkner, AiG)

---

## Hidden Assumptions (Lisle)

![](pics/HiddenPremises.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem")

* The argument silently assumes:
  * The speed of light has always been **constant**
  * Time flows at the **same rate** everywhere
  * One particular **clock synchronization**
  * **Naturalism**: the stars' light arrived by ordinary processes during a supernatural week
* If any assumption fails, the argument fails
* Five proposed answers follow – with their **open problems**

---

## Answer 1: Mature Creation

![](pics/Pinochio.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem")

* Adam was made an adult, trees with fruit – so light may have been created **already on its way**
* Strength: God did create a functioning, mature world
* **Open problem**: we see **events** in the light – exploding stars (SN 1987A), eclipsing binaries in Andromeda
* Light made in transit would show events that **never happened** – God painting fiction
* That seems out of character for God; most creation scientists do not favor this view

---

## Answer 2: C-Decay

![](pics/Lightning.jpg style="float: right; width: 11rem")

* Barry Setterfield (1981): historic measurements show the speed of light **decreasing**
* Best fit: exponential decay, nearly infinite speed in the creation week
* **Open problems** (raised by creation scientists):
  * The early measurements had the largest errors – corrected, they match today's value
  * c depends on basic constants of free space; changing it would change **atoms** themselves
  * No evidence of such a change has been found

---

## Answer 3: Relativistic Models

![](pics/BackToTheFuture.jpg style="float: right; width: 11rem")

* Einstein: gravity slows time (measured with atomic clocks)
* Russell Humphreys, *Starlight and Time* (1994): a **white hole** cosmology – earth deep in a gravity well, so billions of years pass out there while days pass here
* Requires a finite universe with us near its **center**
* **Open problems**: many math details remain; Humphreys himself moved on to a second and a third model; John Hartnett also abandoned his variant

---

## Answer 4: The Synchrony Convention (ASC)

![](pics/ChronoLogical.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

* Jason Lisle: only the **round-trip** speed of light can be measured
* The one-way speed is a **convention** (Einstein chose "same both ways")
* ASC: light toward the observer is instantaneous, away at half speed – every experiment gives the same result
* Like a flight west across time zones: leaves 4:00, lands 4:00 local time
* Genesis speaks from the earth's surface: stars made on Day 4 are **seen** on Day 4

---

## ASC: Strengths and Open Problems

![](pics/Arguments.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

* Strength: physically equivalent to the standard convention – no new physics, no deception
* Astronomers already use "local time" (SN 1987A "happened" in 1987)
* Hartnett, once a relativistic-model author, came to embrace it
* **Open problems**: a convention describes; it does not **explain** – it chooses the clock rather than moving the light
* Critics also ask why distant galaxies look different from near ones if all are the same age

---

## Answer 5: Faulkner's "Dasha" Solution

![](pics/GodCanDoAll.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

<p style="font-family: 'EB Garamond', serif; font-style: italic;">"Then God said, 'Let the earth **bring forth** grass, the herb that yields seed, and the fruit tree that yields fruit according to its kind, whose seed is in itself, on the earth'; and it was so."</p><p style="text-align: right; margin-top: -1.25em; margin-bottom: -0.75em;"> <em>Genesis 1:11 (NKJV)</em></p>

* Creation week involved rapid **processes** – plants grew up, Adam formed from dust
* So on Day 4 God **miraculously brought the light** to earth the same day

---

## Dasha: Strengths and Open Problems

![](pics/GodPower.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

* Named for the Hebrew verb "bring forth" (dasha) in Genesis 1:11
* Strength: fits the text – creation week was supernatural; no painted fictional events
* **Open problems**: a miracle cannot be tested by science
* Faulkner is candid: is there evidence for **any** of these solutions? "Probably not"
* His verdict: for a God big enough to make the universe, light by Day 4 is "**small potatoes**"

---

## Five Answers at a Glance

![](pics/EvidenceScales.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

* **Mature creation** – simple; but shows events that never happened
* **C-decay** – elegant; but data and physics are against it
* **Relativistic models** – real physics; but unfinished, revised by their authors
* **ASC** – no new physics; but a convention, not an explanation
* **Dasha** – fits the text; but a miracle, not testable
* No consensus yet – research continues

---

## The Big Bang Has a Starlight Problem Too

![](pics/ScaleImbalance.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem")

* The cosmic microwave background is the same temperature in all directions (1 part in 100,000)
* Same temperature needs **exchange of light** – but opposite regions were never in contact within 13.8 billion years
* This is the **horizon problem** – a light-travel-time problem
* The fix is inflation – no direct evidence, no known mechanism
* So distant starlight cannot decide between the Bible and the Big Bang

# Stars and Heavy Elements

---

## The Secular Story of Stars

![](pics/ScienceBooks.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem")

* The Big Bang can make only **hydrogen, helium, and trace lithium**
* The other 90 or so elements must come from stars: fusion in cores, then **supernova** explosions
* So stars form from collapsing gas clouds, die, and seed later generations
* Textbooks present this as settled: "All stars begin their lives as parts of nebula"

---

## Can Gas Clouds Make Stars?

![](pics/GodSpheres.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

* Gas clouds are light-years across – far larger than a stable star; gas pressure makes them **expand**, not collapse
* The complete birth of a star has **never been observed** (Don DeYoung, Faulkner)
* "The truth is that we don't understand star formation at a fundamental level" – Abraham Loeb, Harvard
* "Star nurseries": the Horsehead Nebula was measured **expanding**

---

## Missing First-Generation Stars

![](pics/IdentityOverTime.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

* If heavy elements came from earlier stars, the first stars had **none** ("Population III")
* Some should still shine today – their lifespan exceeds the Big Bang age
* Over 100 billion stars in our galaxy – **not one** is free of heavy elements
* Supernova remnants we see appear young (Crab Nebula: seen 1054)
* Consistent with stars **made with** heavy elements from the start
