# "No death before the Fall"? The importance of the distinction of nephesh chayyah life

Source: https://creation.com/no-death-before-the-fall
Author: Lita Sanders (Cosner)
Date: 2012
Stance: YEC

## Key points

- Biblical creationists mean no death of nephesh chayyah ("living soul") creatures before the Fall.
- Plants are not nephesh life in the Hebrew sense, so plants being eaten (Genesis 1:29-30) is not "death".
- The original diet was vegetarian for humans and animals (Genesis 1:29-30); meat eating was permitted after the Flood (Genesis 9:3).
- "Very good" (Genesis 1:31) rules out suffering, predation, and disease in the original creation.
- Romans 5:12 and 8:20-22 tie death and creation's groaning to Adam's sin.
