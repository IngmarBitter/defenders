- Based on the above definitions, I must conclude that the “Origin of Species” is a book about Natural and Human History, not Science. Darwin was acting in the role of Historian and not Scientist.

## Slide 5

- Things to keep in mind
- History has a lesser degree of confidence than Science.
- Science alone cannot tell me what happened in the past.
- Running the laws of Science backward isn’t good enough.
- Science by its very nature, describes how the universe behaves when no intelligent, free-will agents are acting.
- It cannot predict what will have if an intelligent agent gets involved in nature.
- Therefore, when I consider the past, I must take into account the possibility of intelligent agency; otherwise I may never be able to come to the right answer.
