## Slide 5

- What is a fact?
- Something that actually exists or has really occurred.
- A fact is an instance of reality.
- In Science, we start with the reality of the universe.
- A fact is one part of that reality.
- Fact is distinguishable from conclusions or opinions. These are products of the mind.
- In the most basic sense, a scientific fact is an objective and verifiable observation; in contrast with a hypothesis or theory, which is intended to explain or interpret facts.
- I don’t prove a fact. I show you facts.
- It is the “known.”

## Slide 6

- The Answer?

- Because I was not there to observe them.
- One of these may be true.
- But they are not facts in themselves.
- They only express what I believe to be true.

## Slide 14

- Theories
- Theories which have never failed any tests are often called scientific laws, or natural laws.
- Of course, all theories, including those called scientific laws, can always be replaced by more accurate statements if a disagreement with observed data is ever found.
- Progress in science comes when we make a discovery for which existing theories cannot explain, which inspires new or a modified hypothesis.

## Slide 15

- I discovered a new definition of “Fact”
- A fact is a hypothesis that is so firmly supported by evidence that we assume it is true, and act as if it were true. Douglas Futyuma..
- There is no sharp line between speculation, hypothesis, theory, principle, and fact, but only a difference along a sliding scale, in the degree of probability of the idea. When we say a thing is a fact, then, we only mean that its probability is an extremely high one: so high that we are not bothered by doubt about it and are ready to act accordingly. Now in this use of the term fact, the only proper one, evolution is a fact. H.J. Mueller

## Slide 16

- National Academy of Sciences..
- Scientists most often use the word "fact" to describe an observation.
- But scientists can also use fact to mean something that has been tested or observed so many times that there is no longer a compelling reason to keep testing or looking for examples.
- The occurrence of evolution in this sense is fact.
- Scientists no longer question whether descent with modification occurred because the evidence is so strong.

## Slide 18

- Post-Modern Thinking
- Pre-modernist: I call the balls and strikes as they are.
- There is objective reality. I faithfully transmit that to you.
- Modernist: I call the balls and strikes as I see them.
- There is objective reality. But I may alter it as I transmit it to you.
- Post-modernist: There are only balls and strikes when I call them.
- There is no objective reality. I define it.

## Slide 19

- Post-Modern thinking
- This appears to be “post-modern” thinking creeping into Science.
- If a “fact” is only a high degree of my confidence, then I am defining reality.
- It detaches me from the real world.
- The universe no longer defines reality.
- The scientist is now the definer of reality.
- This can lead to mass manipulation, because I stop trusting my own observations and depend on someone else’s confidence.

## Slide 20

- Post-Modern thinking
- If the universe defines reality. And if God created the universe. Then God defines reality.
- However, if it is my confidence that defines reality, then I am taking the place of God.

## Slide 21

- Why is this happening?
- They are confusing the word “fact” with the word “true.”
- My theories may be true, but they are not facts in themselves.
- Richard Dawkins says: “Look at the evidence and you will see that my theory is fact.”
- Notice… He does not say that my theory is plausible or the best explanation.
- So he is not using the language that we have long associated with scientists

## Slide 22

- So to restate this issue
- It is the evidence that are the facts, not my theory.
- It is the universe that defines reality, not my theories about them.
- True science says that my theories may wrong. This allows progress.
- However, if my theories are facts, there is no longer any progress.
