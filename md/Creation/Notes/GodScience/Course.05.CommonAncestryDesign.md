- Common Ancestry / Design

  - Tree of life topology according to
    - Adult appearances
    - Growth appearances

## Slide 3

- Tree of Life Topology Testing
- Appearance: 5 digit hands / feet
  - Adult: same for amphibians and mammals
  - Growth:
    - Human
      - inward (gaps in crease)
    - Frog
      - Outward (digits lengthen)
  - Thus same result, but different growth
  - DNA encodes both
    - Un-common descent is better explanation
