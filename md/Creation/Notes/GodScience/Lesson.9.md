## Slide 9

- What are the elements of written language?
- A language is a particular kind of system for encoding and decoding information.
- A key property of language is that its symbols are arbitrary
- they are merely an agreed-upon convention to represent a certain thing by users of that language
- The meanings of signs may be arbitrary, but the process of assigning meaning is not
- it is the activity of the entire society; individuals are not allowed to change them arbitrarily, even though they may contribute some new meanings
