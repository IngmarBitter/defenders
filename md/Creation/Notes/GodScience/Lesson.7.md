## Slide 14

- Evolutionist Kenneth Miller says…
- He says: “…the bacterial flagellum is not irreducibly complex”
- He says: “Remember the claim that "any precursor to an irreducibly complex system that is missing a part is by definition nonfunctional?“
- A precursor is an evolutionary ancestor
- Above statement an example of the “strawman argument”. He misquotes definition of irreducible complexity
- He says: “..nature is filled with examples of precursors to the flagellum that are indeed "missing a part," and yet are fully-functional.”
- Again this is an example of the “strawman argument”. He misquotes definition of irreducible complexity
- “Type III Secretory System [pump] shows that the bacterial flagellum is not irreducibly complex”
- This is an example of a “red-herring” argument. A molecular pump has nothing to do with a molecular motor. They are both irreducibly complex devices.
