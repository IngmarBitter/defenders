## Slide 3

- *[image]*
- Irresistible Grace - Definition
- Initially everyone resists the will of God.
- Then, for the elect only, the Holy Spirit changes our heart & will, so that we embrace Christ joyfully
- This change cannot be resisted. It is 100% effective. We are “forced” to have faith, but also changed to love it.
- We perceive it as coming from ourselves

## Slide 4

- *[image]*
- God Draws Us
- Calvin: Preaching the Gospel and commanding to repent and believe is an outward general call that has no effect. Only the special inward call to the elect only has an effect that is 100% certain.
  - There is no verse backing this claim

## Slide 5

- *[image]*
- God is Sovereign
- “Irresistible Grace” means “not free will”
- Must be because of His being sovereign
  - Cannot because without the inward call the outward call is being resisted
- God doesn’t become “less sovereign” simply because He is “less controlling” of a creature’s choice

## Slide 6

- God Draws Us
- We cannot come to Christ unless God draws us
- John 6:44, 65
    - No one can come to Me unless the Father who sent Me draws him; and I will raise him up on the last day.
    - Then He said, “For this reason I have said to you that no one can come to Me unless it were given him by My Father.”
      - Agreed, God must be involved
      - The question is if His grace and drawing is resistible or not
      - Traditional: yes, any time
      - Calvin: yes, until He makes it irresistible

## Slide 9

- Repentance
- Repentance is a gift from God
  - Is spiritual
    - Only possible after regeneration
  - 2 Timothy 2:24–26
    - The Lord’s bond-servant must not be quarrelsome, but be kind to all, able to teach, patient when wronged, with gentleness correcting those who are in opposition, if perhaps God may grant them repentance leading to the knowledge of the truth, and they may come to their senses and escape from the snare of the devil, having been held captive by him to do his will.
