## Slide 2

- Definitions (Monergism – One Will)
- Fatalism
  - God decides fate
  - Cause-effect does not exist
- Determinism
  - God decides His plan
  - Cause-effect exists
  - Causes are planned
- Compatibilism
  - God decides His plan
  - Man decides by his own desires
  - Man is responsible for his actions
  - God planned man’s desires

## Slide 3

- Definitions – Free Will
- Libertarian Free Will
  - People can really choose to do otherwise
  - God does not directly affect peoples mind (most of the time)
  - Things happen as people decide
  - Cause-effect exist
  - Man is responsible for consequences of his actions
  - God may convict of sin

## Slide 4

- Definitions - Sovereign
- Sovereign
  - Possessing supreme ruling power
  - Can not be overruled
  - God is sovereign
  - God can decide to create the world with
    - Fatalism
    - Determinism
    - Compatibilism
    - Libertarian Free Will
  - … maybe more attributes,but these all agree upon

## Slide 5

- Definitions - Omniscience
- Omniscience
  - “All-Knowing”
  - God knows all there is to know
  - Facts of Past & Present & Future
  - Molinism: + counter factuals(what would be true if circumstances would be different)
  - Open Theism: minus ‘Future’(God can plan for different possible futures, but does not know which option will become real)

## Slide 6

- *[image]*
- Def. – Foreknowledge & Providence
- Foreknowledge
  - “God’s knowing of the future”
  - Including man’s decisions
  - Foreknowing an event/decisionis not causing the event/decision
- Providence
  - “God’s plan for the world”
  - Decided by God before creation
  - By His will for His Glory
  - Many different plans / worlds possible
  - God could choose based on foreknowledge of human decisions
    - Calvinism: God did not base his plan on foreknowledge
    - Free Will:  God did        base his plan on foreknowledge
