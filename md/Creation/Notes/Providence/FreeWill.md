## Slide 15

- *[image]*
- John Calvin 1509-1564
- Age 8 (1517): Luther’s 95 theses
- Age 22: Studied Latin, Geek, Theology and a degree in Law
- Embraced Luther’s teachings that had reached France
- Age 27 (1536): fled France => Geneva after converting to Protestantism and writing “The Institutes of the Christian Religion”
- Age 29: fled Geneva => Strasbourg after disagreeing with city council
- Eventually back to Geneva, establish a theocracy with social welfare so effective that Geneva had no beggars
- He preached or thought theology every day
- John Knox in 1554: “Geneva is the most perfect school of Christ that ever was in the earth since the days of the apostles.”
- When too old to walk, he was carried to the preaching pulpit
- When on doctor’s bed rest he preached from his bed
- The essence of his teaching is in the acronym T.U.L.I.P.

## Slide 16

- *[image]*
- 5 Point Calvinism: T.U.L.I.P.
- T - Total Depravity / Total Inability
  - Everyone inherits sin guilt from Adam
    - => Babies go to hell
  - No-one unsaved can understand the gospel
- U - Unconditional Election
  - God chooses who is saved (the elect) without any criteria that people can influence
- L - Limited Atonement
  - Jesus died only for the elect
- I - Irresistible Grace
  - The Holy Spirit convicts only the elect and in a way that they must repent
    - => rejection is impossible
- P - Preservation of the Saints
  - Once saved – always saved
    - => Apostasy is impossible

## Slide 39

- *[image]*
- T – Total Depravity / Total Inability
- Conclusion
  - “Fallen man is utterly unable to understand the gospel (deaf & blind)” is a part of the doctrine of “total depravity” that is not actually supported by scripture. Instead scripture supports:
    - Fallen man is born with body and spirit incl. physical and spiritual faculties. However fallen man is born without an active relationship with God/Jesus and without the desire to establish it or to seek out truth about God/Jesus.  When presented with spiritual truth, especially the Gospel then intellectual understanding is possible, but there is no change in attitude possible unless it is paired with an act of the Holy Spirit to convict the sinner of his guiltiness and need for salvation.  Given that conviction, some people accept the Gospel and others still continue to reject it.
