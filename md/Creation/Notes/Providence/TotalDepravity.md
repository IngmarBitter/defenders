This affects everyone, but some are more evil than others.

# Conclusion

“Fallen man is utterly unable to understand the gospel (deaf & blind)” is a part of the doctrine of “total depravity” that is not actually supported by scripture. Instead scripture supports:

## Fallen Nature:

Fallen man is born with body and spirit incl. physical and spiritual faculties. However fallen man is born without an active relationship with God/Jesus and without the desire to establish it or to seek out truth about God/Jesus. When presented with spiritual truth, especially the Gospel then there is no change in attitude possible unless it is paired with an act of the Holy Spirit to convict the sinner of his guiltiness and need for salvation. Given that conviction some people accept the Gospel and others still continue to reject it.
