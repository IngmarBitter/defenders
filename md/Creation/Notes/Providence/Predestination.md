# Free Will

*Free Will is making decisions based on one's heart's desire, without coercion by something or someone. A free will decision feels like what we want, not want someone else wants. Nevertheless, multiple conflicting layers of one's heart's desires can result in free will decisions for which some desire is not met, since other heart's desired appeared stronger and were overriding in the free will decision making process.*

For example, if I could choose any one car, no matter how costly, my multiple layers of desire would be:

- get a BMW Z4 (just released) - the most fun for me

- get a BMW M6 convertible - so not only Joviette but also the kids can come with me

- get a BMW X5 - again we all fit (even the grandparents), and Joviette gets her dream car

Most likely I'd choose the X5 7-seat edition so I can make the most people I love happy, even though that would mean I am not getting the car I would choose if I had no family. Nonetheless, since I'd consider the X5 the best option it would be a free will decision to choose it.

We know that God is sovereign and has a plan for the universe and for each of us; does that mean we do not have free will, but are just playing a predetermined program? No!

# We have Free Will

Without free will, a lot of core theology would not make sense:

- Without free will all we do is "pre-programmed", so committing sins would be "pre-programmed" as well and therefore God's fault or at least according to His plan, so He could not discipline/punish us for it.

- There are many examples or people in the Bible that had to take responsibility for their actions before God, indicating that they were the source of the actions, i.e. that they had free will.

# To fulfill His plan God can shape our desires 

God can shape circumstances (outer or in our hearts) so people's heart's desire is for what He wants them to decide what to do. This can be in form of hardening ones heart or in form of placing specific positive desires into our heart. In either case we do not perceive the process of desire shaping as coercion, and thus still feel like we are acting out of free will, but simultaneously we do what is God's plan.

# Arminian & Calvinist

Up to here Armininans and Calvinists can agree 100%. However, there is one technicality where the views differ with profound implications.

# Is our desire to love God distributed un-equally because of us or because of God?

> Being a child of God through trust in Jesus and his sacrifice on the cross to die in our place is a gift similar to wealth, health, skills, etc., since it is clearly not distributed equally (some become children of God, others do not), but here the inequality has eternal impact

- It is a divine appointed opportunity in concert with a free will decision to take advantage of the opportunity to follow one's heart's desire.

| **Arminian** | **Calvinist** |
|----|----|
| All are given the opportunity | Only the elect are given the opportunity |
| God does not shape our desire for Him | God places the desire for Him in the heart's of the elect |
| Some decide yes some no | All the elect decide yes |
| *We are in control of our heart's desire for God* | *God is in control of our heat's desire for Him, and according to His plan he places only for the elect the desire to love Him* |

# Assurance of salvation

Can we be confident that we will make it to heaven?\
Is there any difference to the assurance of salvation between Aminians and Calvinists?

There are a number of verses that sound on first read like salvation may not be guaranteed, but could depend on what we do as believers:

***John 8:31*** So Jesus said to the Jews who had believed Him, "**If** you **continue in My word**, you really are My disciples.

***John 15:6*** **If** anyone does not **remain in Me**, he is thrown aside like a branch and he withers. They gather them, throw them into the fire, and they are burned.

***Matthew 10:22*** You will be hated by everyone because of My name. But the one who **endures to the end** will be delivered.

***Matthew 24:13*** But the one who **endures to the end** will be delivered.

***Acts 13:43*** After the synagogue had been dismissed, many of the Jews and devout proselytes followed Paul and Barnabas, who were speaking with them and persuading them to **continue** **in** **the grace** of God.

***Romans 2:6*** He will repay each one **according to his works**: ***7*** eternal life to those who by patiently doing good seek for glory, honor, and immortality;

***Romans 11:22*** Therefore, consider God's kindness and severity: severity toward those who have fallen, but God's kindness toward you -- **if** you **remain** **in His kindness**. Otherwise you too will be cut off.

***Colossians 1:23*** **if** indeed you **remain** grounded and steadfast in the faith, and are not shifted away from the hope of the gospel that you heard.

***Hebrews 3:6*** But Christ was faithful as a Son over His household, whose household we are **if** we **hold on** **to the courage** and the confidence of our hope.

***1 Corinthians 16:22*** If anyone does not love the Lord, a curse be on him. Maranatha!

***Hebrews 3:14*** For we have become companions of the Messiah **if** we **hold** firmly **until the end** the reality that we had at the start.

***2 Tim 2:12*** **if** we **endure**, we will also reign with Him; if we deny Him, He will also deny us;

- All of these talk about continuing/remaining/enduring working/holding on to faith/God/Jesus until the end as indicators for really being saved.

There are other verses that suggest that salvation cannot be lost:

***2 Tim 2:13*** if we are faithless, He remains faithful, for He cannot deny Himself.

***1 Peter 1:3*** Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ. According to His great mercy, He **has given us a new birth** **into a living hope** through the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead, ***4*** and into **an inheritance that is imperishable, uncorrupted, and unfading, kept in heaven for you**, ***5*** who are **being protected by God's power** through faith **for** a **salvation** that is ready to be revealed in the last time. ***6*** You rejoice in this, though now for a short time you have had to be distressed by various trials ***7*** so that the genuineness of **your faith** -- more valuable than gold, which perishes though **refined by fire** -- may result in praise, glory, and honor at the revelation of Jesus Christ.

***Hebrews 6:9*** Even though we are speaking this way, dear friends, in your case we are **confident of** the better things connected with **salvation**. \[...\] ***19*** We have this \[**hope**\] -- like **a sure and firm anchor** of the soul

***Ephesians 1:3*** Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who has blessed us with every spiritual blessing in the heavens, in Christ; ***4*** for **He chose us in Him**, before the foundation of the world, **to be holy and blameless in His sight**. In love ***5*** He predestined us to be adopted through Jesus Christ for Himself, according to His favor and will, ***6*** to the praise of His glorious grace that He favored us with in the Beloved. ***7*** In Him we have redemption through His blood, the forgiveness of our trespasses, according to the riches of His grace ***8*** that He lavished on us with all wisdom and understanding. ***9*** He made known to us the mystery of His will, according to His good pleasure that He planned in Him ***10*** for the administration of the days of fulfillment -- to bring everything together in the Messiah, both things in heaven and things on earth in Him. ***11*** In Him we were also made His inheritance, predestined according to the purpose of the One who works out everything in agreement with the decision of His will, ***12*** so that we who had already put our hope in the Messiah might bring praise to His glory. ***13*** **In Him you also**, when you heard the word of truth, the gospel of your salvation -- in Him when you believed -- **were sealed with** **the** promised **Holy Spirit**. ***14*** He is the down payment of our inheritance, for the redemption of the possession, to the praise of His glory. \[...\] ***4:30*** And don't grieve God's **Holy Spirit**, who **sealed you** **for** the day of **redemption**.

***Philippians 1:6*** I am sure of this, that He who started a good work in you will carry it on to completion until the day of Christ Jesus.

***Romans 5:9*** Much more then, since we have now been declared righteous by His blood, we **will be saved** through Him from wrath. ***10*** For if, while we were enemies, we were reconciled to God through the death of His Son, \[then how\] much more, having been reconciled, **will we be saved** by His life!

***Romans 8:38*** For I am persuaded that **neither death nor life, nor angels nor rulers, nor things present, nor things to come, nor powers, *39* nor height, nor depth, nor any other created thing will have the power to separate us from the love of God** that is in Christ Jesus our Lord!

- All of these talk about our salvation/faith/hope/redemption being sure/sealed/firmly anchored by God/Jesus/Holy Spirit

***Exodus 32:32*** Now if You would only forgive their sin. But if not, please **erase me from the book** You have written." ***33*** The Lord replied to Moses: "Whoever has sinned against Me **I will erase from My book**.

How can these three sets of verses be reconciled?
