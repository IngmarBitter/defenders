## Slide 3

- Unconditional Election
- Before creating the world God, chose (elected) individually, which people will be going to heaven. He did so without any human-dependent criteria (condition), i.e. without considering His foreknowledge of people’s believes or will.
  - “Of the eternal election, by which God has predestinated some to salvation, and others to destruction.”
  - “All are not created on equal terms, but some are preordained to eternal life, others to eternal damnation;”
    - Calvin: Institutes of the Christian Religion (Book 3 chapter 21)
  - Implies
    - elected == “will go to heaven”or “unconditionally predestined for permanent salvation”

## Slide 9

- Ephesians 1:4-5
- 4 just as He chose us in Him before the foundation of the world, to be holy and blameless before Him in love; 5 He predestined us to adoption as sons to Himself through Jesus Christ according to the good pleasure of His will. 6 to the praise of the glory of His grace which He graciously bestowed on us in the Beloved.
- Choosing implies individual picking
  - Correct in isolation, but the phrase is “He chose us”, which might as well mean picking a group
- The choosing was decided before people could do anything about/for it
  - Temporal prior does not mean logically prior – God has foreknowledge of the future
- Predestined according to His will, thus not related to our will or choices => Unconditional
  - “Unconditional” is not the logical consequence of “according to His will”, since if God is the one who sets the condition, then it is still according to His will.
- If foreknowledge is just knowing what will happen, and election is conditional, then God does not “choose” people, He merely discovers who matches the criteria. => Not very sovereign
  - God influenced the circumstances of each elected sinner so that they would love Him
- “elected” and predestined in the same verse, but not with the same meaning.

- Romans 8:28-30
- 28 We know that all things work together for good to those who love God, to those who are called according to His purpose. 29 For those whom He foreknew, He predestined to be conformed to the image of His Son, so that He might be the firstborn among many brothers. 30 And those whom He predestined, He also called; and those whom He called, He also justified; and those whom He justified, He also glorified.
  - “Those whom He called, He also justified” implies same set of people, thus all that are called are justified, thus the call cannot be to all people on earth or even all people reading the Bible, but only to the elect
    - Not really, here it only talks about those that eventually become saved. Does not say if others heard the call as well.
- In addition the chain starts with “those who love God” and “those whom he foreknew” which might very well mean “those whom he foreknew would love him” – hence conditional election

## Slide 17

- *[image]*
- Conclusion
- The word elected has many meanings
- There is no verse to support
  - elected == “will go to heaven”or == “unconditionally predestined for permanent salvation”
- There is support that
  - The Jews were called elected
  - In the NT, who starts to believe can be called elected
  - Elected to believe is tied to the condition of human free cooperation which is foreknown but not caused by God
- Reaching for God is possible but not forced (might reach)
- Finding is conditional on reaching for Him
