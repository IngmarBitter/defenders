## Slide 2

- Substitutionary Atonement
- John 3:16 “For God so loved the world that He gave His only begotten Son, that whoever believes in Him should not perish, but have eternal life.
  - Jesus died as a substitute for us
  - His death instead of ours
    - Physical or Spiritual? – the latter
      - The wages of sin is death
      - My God why have you forsaken me?
  - His punishment instead of ours
    - Finite or Infinite? – the latter
      - Infinite time of finite pain vs. finite time of infinite value and infinite pain
    - Unfinished or Finished? – the latter
      - After the sentence is served the prisoner must be released: Jesus was raised
- Belief in and trust in Christ is the basis for becoming a Christian
- As Christian our continuing status is “in Christ” and by virtue of that  status Jesus sacrifice is applied to us continuously
  - New sins are covered by this as we commit them – hence they do not cosign us to hell
  - New sins do degrade the harmony in our relationship with Christ
  - Confession and repentance restore the harmony
  - Continued, habitual new sins may accumulate relationship degradation to the point of loosing the relationship altogether – especially if the continued sinning is becoming equivalent to rejecting God altogether

## Slide 5

- Limited Atonement
- God sent Jesus to die for the sins of His people to save them.
- His people cover all the earth without distinction but not without exception.
  - Distinction: race, sex, age, belief – all not a factor
  - Exception: His people are the only ones Jesus died for, i.e. Jesus did not die for those that God choose not to save.

## Slide 6

- Can I tell my unsaved friend:God loves you so much, He sent Jesus to die for you so that your sins can be forgiven and you can have eternal life in heaven?
  - No, we can not say that, since we do not know who will believe. And Jesus did NOT die for those that do not.
  - Yes, we can say that, since God and Jesus desired for all to be saved and Jesus death provides the means for all. It is only us that reject the offer that limit the effect
- John 3:16
- 16 “For God so loved the world that He gave His only begotten Son, that whoever believes in Him should not perish, but have eternal life.
  - John 3:16 cannot mean that Jesus saved everyone, since clearly not everyone is a Christian.
  - The clear limiting condition is: whoever believes in Him.
  - Does that limit also mean that God has the Love to send Jesus only for those that believe in Him? Yes/No

## Slide 8

- 4 Point Calvinism Inconsistency
- Jesus substitutional sacrifice was surely effective for all that it was intended for
- It was intended only for “to be Christians”
- God unconditionally elected (U.) and irresistibly called (I.) the “to be Christians”
- Total depravity (T.) means that all others are unable to appreciate Jesus sacrifice
- It makes no sense that Jesus death was for the reprobate (made for hell)
- Believing that the scope was universal thus implies denial of T. U. and I.
- Jesus substitutional sacrifice was intended for those that will freely believe
- Jesus death was intended for all people to help see how much He loves them and as a means to help them to believe

## Slide 9

- When is Jesus sacrifice applied?
- At time of God decreeing His plan
- At time of Jesus sacrificial death
- At time of God regenerating the sinner
- At time of the believer trusting Jesus
  - It cannot be the first two since Jesus died for us when we were still sinners
  - Belief and faith are gifts given to spiritually alive men, hence regeneration must come first
  - Belief is the condition for eternal life, thus it is logically prior to regeneration, but timely both are simultaneous
