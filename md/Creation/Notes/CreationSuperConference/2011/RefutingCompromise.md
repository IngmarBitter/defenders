## Slide 30

- Hebrew narrative
- ‘Usually a narrative begins with a qatal (historic perfect) and continues with a wayyiqtol (waw consecutive), which is followed, if need be, by other wayyiqtols, …’
- Joüon, P. and Muraoka, T., A Grammar of Biblical Hebrew: Part Three: Syntax, pp. 390, Pontifical Biblical Institute, Rome, 1991.
- Genesis 1:
- First verb: בָּרָא  bārā’ (create) — historic perfect
- Then: series of waw consecutives:
- וַיּאמֶר  wāyyō’mer (‘and … said’), וַיְהִי wāyehi (‘and there was’), וַיַּרְא  wāyyāre (‘and … saw’).
