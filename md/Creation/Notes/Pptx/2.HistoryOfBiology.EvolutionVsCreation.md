## Slide 23

- Evolution Counter Examples
- Tree of life not unique
- Irreducible Complex Systems
  - Complicated and can be copied exactly
  - Cannot be reduced to simpler systems
    - Without total functionality loss
    - Humans change 100 base pairs per generation
    - Introducing a new protein takes 1000 base pairs
    - Adapting an existing protein takes 100 base pairs for the new structure and a lot of extra ones to coordinate proper manufacturing time, place and amount as well as transport to the right place
    - 1000 mutations in a row need to be matched 1:4e1000 or 1:10e602 – much worse than 1:10e35
