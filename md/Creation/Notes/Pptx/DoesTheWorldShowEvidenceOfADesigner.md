## Slide 7

- Fine-Tuning in Physics
- Atoms must be able to form to allow life
  - Strong nuclear force glues protons and neutrons
    - If slightly greater (0.3% stronger) all protons and neutrons would bind together. There would be only heavy elements in the universe, but no life.
    - If slightly weaker (2%) protons and neutrons would not stick together. Only one element, hydrogen would exist in the universe, but no life.

## Slide 8

- Fine-Tuning in Physics
- Force of gravity
  - Equation could stay the same, but constant G could be different
  - The value of G determines how hot the stars burn
  - If the stronger, stars would burn up too quickly and erratically for life to exist
  - If weaker, stars wouldn't be hot enough for nuclear fusion; no heavy elements would be produced.

## Slide 10

## Slide 11

- Fine-Tuning in Physics
- Neutrons are and must be 0.138% more massive than protons
  - If neutrons were an additional 0.1% more massive, there wouldn't be enough of them to make heavy elements necessary for life.
  - If neutrons were 0.1% less massive, protons would more rapidly decay into neutrons and all the stars in the universe would have collapsed.
- The number of electrons and protons must be equal
  - If not balanced, galaxies, stars and planets would have never formed because electromagnetic forces would have overcome gravitational forces.
