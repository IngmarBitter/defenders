# "Ape-Men": What the Fossils Show

---

## A Track Record of Mistakes

![](pics/PoliticsOverScience.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

* **Java Man** (1891): a skullcap and a thighbone found 45 feet apart, joined into one "missing link" – two different creatures
* **Piltdown Man** (1912): "Darwin Theory Proved True" (*New York Times*) – in 1953 exposed as a fraud: a human skull with an ape jaw
* **Nebraska Man** (1922): used in the 1925 Scopes trial – an extinct **pig's tooth**
* **Ramapithecus**: "ideally structured to be an ancestor of hominids" (*Time*, 1977) – now regarded as an ape related to the orangutan
* In each case, the claimed age of the "missing link" was wrong too

---

## All-Ape or All-Human

![](pics/EvidenceScales.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

* The "ape-men" fossils fall into **two groups**:
  * **Ape-like:** Ardipithecus, Australopithecus, Paranthropus, Homo habilis, Homo rudolfensis
  * **Human:** Homo erectus/ergaster, Homo heidelbergensis, Neanderthals, Homo sapiens
* Ardipithecus, Australopithecus, Paranthropus are too different from both man and chimp to be ancestors of either – side branches
* The charts show a smooth line – the fossils show **extinct apes and extinct humans**, but no common ancestors

---

## Lucy: An Ape

![](pics/ScaleWithQuestions.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem")

* Lucy (Australopithecus afarensis), found 1974: about 40% of a skeleton
* Long arms, jaws, and upper leg bone like a chimpanzee
* Brain 400–500 cc – within the chimpanzee range
* Long curved fingers and toes, strong back muscles – a **tree-dweller**
* "The ape-like profile of Australopithecus is so pronounced that its outline can be superimposed on that of a female chimpanzee" (Joseph Weiner)

---

## Did Lucy Walk Upright?

![](pics/Logic.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

* **Ribs:** conical like an ape's, not barrel-shaped like a man's (Peter Schmid)
* **Pelvis:** "the marked resemblance of AL 288-1 (Lucy) to the chimpanzee is equally obvious" (Stern and Susman)
* **Wrist:** "she had the morphology that was classic for **knuckle walkers**" (Richmond and Strait, *Nature* 2000)
* **Inner ear:** CT scans of the balance canals of australopithecines resemble the great apes (Fred Spoor)
* Upright walking needs a whole package at once – balance, skull, back, hips, knees, arched feet, strong big toes (Stuart Burgess)

---

## The Laetoli Footprints

![](pics/BibleAndScienceBooks.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

* Footprints in volcanic ash at Laetoli, Tanzania (1978), dated at 3.5 million years
* "Indistinguishable from those of habitually barefoot Homo sapiens" (Russell Tuttle)
* Modern heel, strong arch, big toe in line (Johanson) – yet assigned to Lucy's kind, because in the evolutionary timeline humans did not yet exist
* Textbooks and museum statues give Lucy **human feet** – the evidence is fitted to the story

---

## Neanderthals: Fully Human

![](pics/HumanCondition.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem")

* First drawn as stooped ape-men – the reconstructions were wrong
* Brain larger than ours; they buried their dead, used jewelry and musical instruments, painted caves, and could speak
* Buried **together with modern humans** at four sites (Marvin Lubenow)
* Heavy brow and stocky build: an isolated people in a harsh, cold climate
* Homo erectus and Heidelberg Man also look **human in all traits** – Erectus even built boats
* Here the Creation side and Craig agree: **Neanderthals were human**

# The 98% Claim

---

## Where the 98% Came From

![](pics/ManipulateStatistics.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

* 1975: Wilson and King, **DNA hybridization** – a crude method that measured only DNA that was already similar
* Later sequencing studies compared **preselected** highly similar genes – the "98–99% gold standard" was kept
* Any two random DNA strands are already **25%** identical – there are only four letters
* Jeffrey Tomkins and Jerry Bergman (CMI) reviewed the published data itself

---

## What the 98% Leaves Out

![](pics/dna-art.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

* Britten counted indels and got 95% – add the DNA his alignments left out and it is about **87%**; most later studies dropped the indels again
* Only about **70%** of the chimp genome could be aligned to the human genome at all
* The chimp genome is about **8% larger** than ours
* 1,480 human genes have no counterpart in the chimp; the Y chromosomes differ dramatically
* Only 20% of compared proteins are identical

---

## Same Parts, Different Design

![](pics/BrainInnate.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

* The biggest differences are in **gene regulation** – which genes are switched on, when, and how much
* In the brain, the differences are enormous – 90% of 169 differently expressed brain genes run higher in humans
* A computer, a camera, and a VCR share transistors and wires – the difference is **how the parts are put together**
* Similar parts point to a **common Designer** using a good plan, not to common descent
* By the same method human DNA is roughly 35% like a daffodil's – we are not 35% daffodil

# The Historical Adam

---

## Craig: Adam Was a Real Man

![](pics/EveReachingForFruit.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem")

* Craig reads Genesis 1–11 as **mytho-history** – but still insists on a real Adam and Eve
* The **genealogies** end in people who were plainly historical
* Craig: Paul's teaching on Adam "bursts the bounds of a purely literary figure" (Man 19; Deck 2)
* So Adam and Eve are **the ancestors of every human being** who has ever lived
* Craig then asks: if Adam was real, **when** did he live?

---

## When Were There Humans?

![](pics/HandsEarth.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem")

* Human means: body like ours, and **modern behavior** – abstract thinking, planning, innovation, symbols (McBrearty and Brooks)
* Latest point: the cave art of Chauvet (30,000 years) and hand stencils of Sulawesi (35,000–40,000) – surely human
* Earliest point: Homo erectus – before him, brains too small (Homo habilis 550–687 cc)
* Craig: humans began between about **1 million and 50,000 years** ago, on the standard dates

---

## Neanderthals Were Human

![](pics/GodCreation.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

* Neanderthals interbred with Homo sapiens – 2% of non-African DNA today
* **Bruniquel Cave**: stalagmite rings built 336 meters deep in the dark – a planned group project
* **Neanderthal cave art** in Spain – symbolic behavior
* **Schöningen spears**: javelins as good as Olympic ones – foresight and planning
* So Neanderthals, and their ancestor **Heidelberg Man**, are part of the human family

---

## Adam as Heidelberg Man

![](pics/GodSittingOnEarth.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem")

* Homo heidelbergensis: brain around 1,260 cc – within the modern range; about 6 feet tall
* The likely common ancestor of Homo sapiens, Neanderthals, and Denisovans
* Craig's proposal: **Adam and Eve were Heidelberg people**, living perhaps more than 750,000 years ago – possibly in the Near East
* Perhaps God caused a change in the brain, and gave them **rational souls**
* "Not some slumping ape-man" – someone who could pass among us

---

## The Genetic Challenge Answered

![](pics/Arguments.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

* Dennis Venema (BioLogos): human genetic variety needs a population of at least **10,000** – no first couple
* Joshua Swamidass and Richard Buggs: population estimates are **averages**, and a couple bottleneck was never tested
* A couple can carry **four** versions of each gene – Swamidass finds such a couple possible about **500,000** years ago
* Venema conceded in 2019: a couple more than 500,000 years ago cannot be ruled out
* Craig: the genetic argument against a first pair fails

---

## Craig's Picture

![](pics/brainPower.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

* A population of several thousand hominins – **animals**, without rational thought
* God selects two, renews their brains, and gives them **rational souls** – only they are truly human
* They sin and bring **spiritual** death; Adam was physically mortal from the start
* Their non-human contemporaries were not in God's image
* Craig: "just one view" – offered to show that a historical Adam and modern science **need not conflict**

# A Young-Earth Response

---

## Much Common Ground

![](pics/BookThink.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

* Craig is right: Adam and Eve were **real people**, the ancestors of all humans
* Right that the gospel needs them (Deck 2: Adam in the New Testament)
* Right that Neanderthals and Heidelberg Man were **fully human**, not ape-men
* Right that a first couple is possible – but his answer covers only a couple older than 500,000 years; a recent Adam needs its own answer (created diversity, measured mutation rates)
* The dispute is not whether Adam lived, but **how** he was made and **when**

---

## Formed from the Dust

![](pics/ChristWork.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

<p style="font-family: 'EB Garamond', serif; font-style: italic;">"And the LORD God **formed man of the dust of the ground**, and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life; and man became a living being."</p><p style="text-align: right; margin-top: -1.25em; margin-bottom: -0.75em;"> <em>Genesis 2:7 (NKJV)</em></p>

* Man is made from dust – not chosen from a tribe of hominins
* Eve is made from Adam (Genesis 2:21–22) – no parents at all
* Adam "the son of God" (Luke 3:38) – no animal father between him and God

---

## The Genealogies Give a Date

![](pics/GodCosmosClock.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem")

<p style="font-family: 'EB Garamond', serif; font-style: italic;">"And Adam lived **one hundred and thirty years**, and begot a son in his own likeness, after his image, and named him Seth."</p><p style="text-align: right; margin-top: -1.25em; margin-bottom: -0.75em;"> <em>Genesis 5:3 (NKJV)</em></p>

* Genesis 5 and 11 give the father's age at each son's birth – a chronology, not just a list
* Adam to Abraham: about 2,000 years; Abraham to Christ: about 2,000
* So Adam lived about **6,000 years ago** – the genealogies Craig uses for Adam's reality also give his date

---

## Why Not Heidelberg Adam?

![](pics/ScaleImbalance.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem")

<p style="font-family: 'EB Garamond', serif; font-style: italic;">"But **from the beginning of the creation**, God 'made them male and female.'"</p><p style="text-align: right; margin-top: -1.25em; margin-bottom: -0.75em;"> <em>Mark 10:6 (NKJV)</em></p>

* Jesus puts man at the **beginning** – not billions of years in
* 750,000 years of death, disease, and fossils **before** Adam's sin – death before the Fall (Deck 2)
* The ages rest on dating methods that are revised when a fossil does not fit (skull 1470, Lucy) – see Deck 4 on discordant dates
