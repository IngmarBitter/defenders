# Creation Unit – Structure Plan

Working doc (plain markdown, md-only, never mirrored to `docs/`). Plans the deck series for Topic 10 "Doctrine of Creation vs Evolutionism" before any slides are written.

## Stance and Method

- **Diamond reading of Genesis 1–11.** The historical/literal, literary (structure, poetry, parallelism), theological/polemic, and moral (Sabbath pattern) facets are all true at once. The historical six-day timeline is one facet of the diamond – not in competition with the others.
- **What is rejected:** readings that must deny the historical timeline, and concordism – force-fitting modern science into Genesis 1 (day-age is the main case).
- **Two tests for every interpretation:**
  - (T1) Exegesis: does it come out of the text, as the author and his first audience would have read it?
  - (T2) Compatibility: can it stand alongside the historical timeline, or must it replace it?
- **Division of sources:**
  - (a) What the text says and which readings make hermeneutical sense – Craig (Series 4 Excursus 1–21) is the main source.
  - (b) How the text relates to science – CMI / AiG / ICR / DI are the main sources (`Sources/`), Craig Excursus 22–30 as the counterpart, RTB and BioLogos as opposing views to steelman.
- **Deck modes** (root.ai.md): the Genesis decks use mode (a) *integration* for the facets that fit together, but the rivals that deny the timeline (gap, day-age, myth-only) are judged as in mode (b). The science decks are evidence decks: state the claim, give the evidence, answer the rival.

### Deck 1 – Creation out of Nothing

- Why the doctrine matters: God is the Creator of everything other than Himself (Craig Creation 1).
- Scripture, cited once:
  - Genesis 1:1 as a title and independent sentence, not "When God began to create" (Creation 2).
  - Other Old Testament texts (Psalms, Isaiah, Proverbs 8).
  - New Testament: John 1:3; Romans 4:17; Colossians 1:16; Hebrews 11:3; Revelation 4:11 (Creation 3).
- Systematic summary – definition: a beginning, no material cause (Creation 4).
- Church fathers break with Greek eternal matter; philosophical and scientific arguments → 1-slide Kalam recap + link (Creation 5).
- Objections: to *ex nihilo* ("nothing comes from nothing") and to *creatio* (a timeless God causing a temporal effect) (Creation 6–7).
- Continuing creation and conservation: the universe depends on God moment by moment (Creation 8–9).
- Takeaways.

- Hermeneutics: exegesis vs eisegesis vs concordism; genre; "when the plain sense makes common sense" (Series2.9.Summary).

  - Mytho-history (Craig), which is stated here but examined in Deck 3
- **Scripture review**, each passage once, with support/challenge bullets per view:

  - Genesis 2:4 (yom broadly)
  - Exodus 20:8–11 and 31:17
  - Mark 10:6
  - Genesis 1:29–30 and 9:3 (plant food)
  - Genesis 2:17, 3:17–19, Romans 5:12, 8:20–22 (death and thorns after the fall)
  - Genesis 5 and 11 (genealogies)
- Uses FatManSpandexYomDay1, GenesisInterpretations, and the day-age timelines.

### Deck 3 – The Days of Genesis: The Diamond

- Takeaways: how to talk with old-earth Christians charitably.
