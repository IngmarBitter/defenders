# Creation Unit – Structure Plan

Working doc (plain markdown, md-only, never mirrored to `docs/`). Plans the deck series for Topic 10 "Doctrine of Creation vs Evolutionism" before any slides are written.

## Stance and Method

- **Diamond reading of Genesis 1–11.** The historical/literal, literary (structure, poetry, parallelism), theological/polemic, and moral (Sabbath pattern) facets are all true at once. The historical six-day timeline is one facet of the diamond – not in competition with the others.
- **What is rejected:** readings that must deny the historical timeline, and concordism – force-fitting modern science into Genesis 1 (day-age is the main case).
- **Two tests for every interpretation:**
  - (T1) Exegesis: does it come out of the text, as the author and his first audience would have read it?
  - (T2) Compatibility: can it stand alongside the historical timeline, or must it replace it?
- **Division of sources:**
  - (a) What the text says and which readings make hermeneutical sense – Craig (Series 4 Excursus 1–21) is the main source.
  - (b) How the text relates to science – CMI / AiG / ICR / DI are the main sources (`Sources/`), Craig Excursus 22–30 as the counterpart, RTB and BioLogos as opposing views to steelman.
- **Deck modes** (root.ai.md): the Genesis deck uses the mode (c) hypothesis-trial layout; its verdicts integrate the facets that fit together (mode (a)) and reject the rivals that deny the timeline (gap, day-age, myth-only). The science decks are evidence decks: state the claim, give the evidence, answer the rival.

### Deck 1 – Creation out of Nothing

- Why the doctrine matters: God is the Creator of everything other than Himself (Craig Creation 1).
- Scripture, cited once:
  - Genesis 1:1 as a title and independent sentence, not "When God began to create" (Creation 2).
  - Other Old Testament texts (Psalms, Isaiah, Proverbs 8).
  - New Testament: John 1:3; Romans 4:17; Colossians 1:16; Hebrews 11:3; Revelation 4:11 (Creation 3).
- Systematic summary – definition: a beginning, no material cause (Creation 4).
- Church fathers break with Greek eternal matter; philosophical and scientific arguments → 1-slide Kalam recap + link (Creation 5).
- Objections: to *ex nihilo* ("nothing comes from nothing") and to *creatio* (a timeless God causing a temporal effect) (Creation 6–7).
- Continuing creation and conservation: the universe depends on God moment by moment (Creation 8–9).
- Takeaways.

### Deck 2 – The Days of Genesis

One big deck in the **hypothesis-trial pattern** (root.ai.md mode (c)): nine rival readings are too many to state up front and judge much later. The Scripture is established once; then each view gets its own adjacent statement → verdict pair, so every view has its own section without re-quoting.

- **Part 1 – How to read Genesis**
  - Exegesis vs eisegesis vs concordism; genre; "when the plain sense makes common sense" (Series2.9.Summary).
  - The two tests: (T1) from the text? (T2) can it stand alongside the historical timeline?
  - **Map of the views: Ingmar's GenesisInterpretations diagram** (`docs/Creation/pics/GenesisInterpretations.png`) – the literal/figurative, accurate/obsolete science, young/old earth, and day = 24h ✓ / ? / ✗ regions, with the evolution-compatible (*) and old-fossil-compatible (+) markers. It serves as the roadmap for Part 3 and returns at the Diamond.
- **Part 2 – The text, once** (each passage quoted only here; later slides refer back):
  - Genesis 1:1–2:3 (yom with ordinals, evening-morning, day 4 light, day 7 without evening)
  - Genesis 2:4 (yom broadly)
  - Exodus 20:8–11 and 31:17
  - Mark 10:6
  - Genesis 1:29–30 and 9:3 (plant food)
  - Genesis 2:17, 3:17–19, Romans 5:12, 8:20–22 (death and thorns after the fall)
  - Genesis 5 and 11 (genealogies)
  - Genesis 7:19–23 (the Flood as text – the geology is in Deck 4)
- **Part 3 – The interpretations on trial**, one section each, following the diagram's regions. Each section is a statement slide (steelmanned) followed by a verdict slide – "What X Gets Right" (the facet it contributes) and/or "Why X Fails" (T1/T2):
  - Literal naive (obsolete science)
  - Literal accurate – YEC (Series2.9.Summary)
  - Verse 1–2 gap (Chalmers, Scofield)
  - Day-gap (Lennox)
  - Day-age (Ross / RTB; Hugh Ross review, Day-Age questions; FatManSpandexYomDay1, day-age timelines)
  - Revelation-day / days of divine proclamation
  - Literary framework (Blocher, Kline) – facet: structure
  - Functional / cosmic temple (Walton) – facet: purpose
  - Creation myth (Miller and Soden) – facet: polemic against ANE myths; ANE parallels, Egyptian and Babylonian (Craig Excursus 9–12)
  - Mytho-history (Craig Excursus 13–20: etiological motifs, genealogies, genre, non-literal elements) – steelman, then answer: figurative *and* historical, not figurative *instead of* historical. On the diagram it straddles the Literal edge in the ✗ column, above Creation Myth.
- **Part 4 – The Diamond**
  - Adam in the New Testament – Jesus and Paul read Genesis as history (Craig Man 12–14; CMI).
  - The Diamond slide: the historical 6 × 24h core with the structural, purposive, polemic and Sabbath facets (fed by the Part 3 verdicts). Reuse the atonement diamond image idea.
  - Central truths of Genesis 1–11 (Craig Excursus 21) – common ground with other Christians.
- **Takeaways** – how to talk with old-earth Christians charitably.

- Takeaways.
