# What "Evolution" Means

---

## Three Senses of One Word

![](pics/ThinkingMan.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem")

* Francisco Ayala (via Craig) distinguishes three aspects of the evolutionary paradigm:
  * **Change over time** – descent with modification; at most, universal common ancestry (UCA)
  * **Evolutionary history** – the tree of life; "a lot, lot, lot to be discovered still"
  * **Mechanisms** – mutation, selection, and more; "still actively investigated"
* Only the first is called a "fact" – and even Ayala says the other two remain uncertain, some "conjectural"

---

## Beware the Switch

![](pics/HiddenPremises.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem")

* Craig: it is **equivocation** to prove sense 1 and then claim sense 3 – that mutation and selection are shown to be adequate
* "Fact" gets redefined: Futuyma – a hypothesis so well supported "we assume it is true" – that is a definition of **faith**
* Descent with modification is observed; molecules-to-man is **extrapolated**
* Chicago conference (1980): can microevolution be extrapolated to macroevolution? "**A clear, No**"
* Darwin's *Origin* is a book of **history** – the question is whose account of the past is right

---

## What We Mean in This Deck

![](pics/TreeDryLush.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem")

* Both sides accept what we observe: **mutation** and **natural selection** – variation within kinds
* They differ on where the kinds came from:
  * **Evolutionism:** mutation + natural selection + **common descent**
  * **Creation:** mutation + natural selection + **common design**
* For the rest of this deck, "evolution" means evolutionism in this sense

# Mutations and Natural Selection

---

## After Their Kind

![](pics/WalkingBear.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem")

<p style="font-family: 'EB Garamond', serif; font-style: italic;">"Then God said, 'Let the earth bring forth the living creature **according to its kind**: cattle and creeping thing and beast of the earth, each according to its kind'; and it was so. And God made the beast of the earth according to its kind, cattle according to its kind, and everything that creeps on the earth according to its kind. And God saw that it was good."</p><p style="text-align: right; margin-top: -1.25em; margin-bottom: -0.75em;"> <em>Genesis 1:24–25 (NKJV)</em></p>

* "According to its kind" – ten times in Genesis 1: variety **within** kinds, not one kind turning into another

---

## DNA: A Proofread Copy

![](pics/Polymerase.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem")

* DNA stores the recipe: triplets encode amino acids; ~330 amino acids per average protein; human genome ~3 billion base pairs
* Copying is zipper-like and **very accurate** – about 1 error per billion letters, with proofreading and repair
* **Mutations** are copy mistakes that slip through
* **Natural selection** removes the worst ones; most small ones are **neutral** and pile up
* So selection mainly **filters** – it explains the survival of the fittest, not the **arrival** of the fittest

---

## What Mutations Actually Do

![](pics/ATCG-list.jpg style="float: right; width: 11rem")

* Most mutations are harmful or neutral – they cause disease
* "Beneficial" ones typically **break** something that happens to help:
  * Flightless island birds; Belgian Blue cattle (broken myostatin control gene)
  * Antibiotic resistance: a pump that no longer takes in the drug, a disabled control gene, a less specific enzyme
  * Sickle hemoglobin protects from malaria – but causes sickle-cell anemia
* Real change, but **loss** of information, not new machinery

---

## The Edge of Evolution

![](pics/PasswordLock.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem")

* Michael Behe, *The Edge of Evolution*: malaria has trillions of cells per patient and mutates fast
* It beat every drug with **one** mutation, rarely two
* In 10,000 years it never overcame sickle hemoglobin – that needs **several coordinated** mutations
* HIV tried every combination of up to six point mutations, yet stayed "a complete stick-in-the-mud"
* So a few coordinated mutations are about the **edge** of what blind search can do

---

## Lenski's Citrate Bacteria

![](pics/Dominos.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

* Lenski grew E. coli for tens of thousands of generations – a showcase: "major evolutionary shift in the lab"
* Around generation 33,000 some began to use **citrate** in the presence of oxygen
* But no new digestion system arose: the ability to digest citrate **was already there**
* The mutations changed **regulation** – a gate that kept citrate out when oxygen was present now lets it in
* Adaptation by rewiring and loss of control, not the arrival of new machines

---

## An Orchard, Not a Single Tree

![](pics/CatDogTalking.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem")

* Not a "lawn" of fixed species – creationists **reject** fixity; Linnaeus himself dropped it
* God created the original **kinds** with great built-in ability to adapt
* Since then mutation and selection produced the many **species** we see – dogs, cats, finch beaks
* Kind (baramin: bara "create" + min "kind"): species that can **hybridize** belong to one kind – liger, zonkey, wholphin
* Often near the level of the **family** – many planted trees, an orchard

# Irreducible Complexity

---

## Darwin's Own Test

![](pics/rube-goldberg-machine.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem")

* Darwin: "If it could be demonstrated that any complex organ existed which could not possibly have been formed by numerous, successive, slight modifications, my theory would absolutely break down"
* Behe (*Darwin's Black Box*, 1996): an **irreducibly complex** system has several well-matched parts, and removing any one stops it working
* Picture a mousetrap: base, spring, hammer, holding bar, catch – no part catches "a few mice" alone
* Selection keeps only what works now – it cannot aim at a machine that works only when complete

---

## The Bacterial Flagellum

![](pics/proteins.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem")

* A rotary **outboard motor**: rotor, stator, drive shaft, propeller – at nanoscale
* Built from about **40 protein parts**, some 30 unique; knockout experiments show that losing one destroys motility
* Assembly is ordered: a pump exports the parts in sequence and they self-assemble
* The parts **and** their assembly instructions must all be coded in DNA
* No stepwise path of functional precursors has been shown

---

## "It Came from a Pump"

![](pics/ScaleWithQuestions.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem")

* Miller and Dawkins: the flagellum evolved from the **Type III secretory system** (T3SS), a protein pump
* But the T3SS shares only about **a quarter** of the flagellar parts, and does not explain the motor
* It serves parasites that attack cells that came later; flagella are far more widespread
* So even evolutionary experts think the pump came **from** the flagellum (Minnich, Nguyen et al.)
* Dembski: finding Hawaii does not show you can walk from Los Angeles to Tokyo

---

## Blood Clotting and Other Machines

![](pics/Orchestra.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem")

* **Blood clotting**: a cascade of proteins, each activating the next – lose one and you bleed (hemophilia) or clot everywhere
* Doolittle and Miller proposed gene-duplication stories; Behe and Luskin: pathways are **sketched, not shown**
* **ATP synthase**: a rotary motor making the cell's energy currency
* **Kinesin**: a walking porter carrying cargo along microtubule roads in 8 nm steps
* **Chaperonins** fold proteins – but who folded the first chaperonins?

---

# The Cambrian Explosion

---

## Darwin's Doubt

![](pics/BookThink.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

* Darwin needed long ages in which "the world swarmed with living creatures" before the Cambrian
* The fossils did not supply them, and he admitted it:
  * "The case at present must remain inexplicable; and may be truly urged as a valid argument against the views here entertained"
* Stephen Meyer, *Darwin's Doubt* (2013): the problem has only grown since

---

## A Real Explosion

![](pics/Seashell.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

* About 20 of the ~26 phyla in the fossil record appear **abruptly**: eyes, legs, heads, guts – even fish (Haikouichthys)
* Erwin and Valentine: "all or nearly all the major phylum-level groups" appear together
* Too small or soft to fossilize? Precambrian rocks preserve even **bacteria**
* The Ediacaran fauna "bore little or no resemblance" to the Cambrian animals – not their ancestors
* Molecular clocks conflict wildly – and assume the ancestors they are meant to find

---

## Where Does the Information Come From?

![](pics/dna-art.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

* New body plans need new genes, proteins, cell types (trilobites need 50+), and regulatory networks
* Douglas Axe: functional protein folds are as rare as **1 in 10<sup>77</sup>** sequences
* Multiple **coordinated** mutations are needed – beyond realistic waiting times
* Early body-plan mutations are **embryonic lethals**; changing regulatory networks breaks development
* Meyer: the only cause known to produce such information is **intelligence**
* On a young-earth view the Cambrian is not an early age but the lowest buried marine life of the Flood (Deck 4)

# Common Ancestry or Common Design

---

## The Best Case for Common Ancestry

![](pics/EvidenceScales.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

* Steelman (Craig, following Dennis Venema): the fossil evidence is weak, but the **molecular** evidence is strong
* Almost all life shares **one genetic code**, though many codes were possible
* Similarity is **deeper than needed**: the same synonymous codons, not just the same amino acids
* Related species share the same **gene order**
* And the same broken genes – **pseudogenes** – in the same places: "Why copy a broken door handle?"

---

## Common Design

![](pics/GodFingerprint.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem")

<p style="font-family: 'EB Garamond', serif; font-style: italic;">"For since the creation of the world His invisible attributes are clearly seen, being understood by **the things that are made**, even His eternal power and Godhead, so that they are without excuse,"</p><p style="text-align: right; margin-top: -1.25em; margin-bottom: -0.75em;"> <em>Romans 1:20 (NKJV)</em></p>

* Designers reuse parts – Ford and GM share design plans; no Chevrolet evolved from a Ford
* One code and shared parts point to **one** Designer, not many

---

## Answering the Molecular Case

![](pics/Science.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem")

* Common design predicts **shared subsets**; common ancestry predicts that all trees agree
* Yet trees conflict: "phylogenetic conflict is common … the norm rather than the exception"
* Cytochrome c: man closer to kangaroo than to horse; insulin: man closer to pig than to chimp
* Paramecium uses a **different code** – a change that would scramble every protein at once
* "Junk DNA" and "pseudogenes" keep turning out to have **regulatory functions** – shared function, not shared scars

---

## Similar Shapes, Different Origins

![](pics/TreeDryLush.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem")

* Five digits: human fingers form by **cell death** between them, frog digits **grow outward**
* Three-toed feet: dinosaurs grow digits 1-2-3, birds 2-3-4
* Alberch: homologous structures forming "from distinctly dissimilar initial states" is "the rule rather than the exception"
* Similar eyes, fins, wings in unrelated groups are explained away as "convergence"
* Meyer: "similarity implies common ancestry, except … in those many, many cases when it does not"

---

## Intermediates Are Not Transitions

![](pics/Fern.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem")

* Craig: an **intermediate** form mixes features; a **transitional** form is the actual bridge
* Archaeopteryx is intermediate, not transitional – birds appear before it
* Millions of transitions are required; they "have not been found because they are not there to be found" (Craig)
* Species appear suddenly, persist unchanged, and vanish – **stasis**, not gradual change (Tattersall, Gould)
* Ayala: "small changes do not accumulate"

---

## "Poof – a Duck?"

![](pics/GodCreated.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem")

* Craig's objection: special creation pictures ducks and dinosaurs popping into being in history – "more magic than the God of the Bible"
* Answer: the young-earth view is not creation scattered over the fossil record – God made the kinds in **one creation week** by His word
* Creating mature creatures is no deception – no more than mature wine at Cana or a grown Adam
* Craig himself grants a miraculous origin of life (Deck 5) – the same God may make kinds

# Theistic Evolution

---

## Evolutionary Creation – Steelmanned

![](pics/BibleAndScienceBooks.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

* BioLogos: "God is the creator and sustainer of all things, and evolution is the best scientific explanation for the relatedness of life"
* Affirms the Trinity, Christ's deity and bodily resurrection, miracles, answered prayer, salvation by grace
* Prefers "evolutionary creation": they are **creationists** first; God works through natural processes
* Accepts common ancestry, humans included; views on Adam, the Fall and Genesis **vary**
* Motive: take both books seriously, and keep science from driving people away from faith

## Why It Undercuts the Gospel

![](pics/Redemption.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem")

<p style="font-family: 'EB Garamond', serif; font-style: italic;">"For the creation was subjected to futility, not willingly, but because of Him who subjected it in hope; because the creation itself also will be delivered from the bondage of corruption [...]"</p><p style="text-align: right; margin-top: -1.25em; margin-bottom: -0.75em;"> <em>Romans 8:20–21 (NKJV)</em></p>

* If Adam was already mortal, the curse changed nothing – "you shall surely die" becomes empty
* No real fall means nothing for Christ to **restore** – the gospel loses its first chapter

# Craig's Integration

---

## Craig: Progressive Creation

![](pics/TwoViews.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

* Genetic evidence supports common ancestry; the fossil evidence tends **against** it
* The mechanisms are **uncertain**: gradualism, new body plans, and the genetic code remain unsolved
* So Craig finds **progressive creation** most attractive: God intervenes at key junctures (mutations, chemical combinations) on existing life
* It would explain both the genetic similarity and the fossil **discontinuity**
* He holds it loosely: "I have no final and fixed viewpoint"

---

## Craig on Flaws and Animal Pain

![](pics/CatEatsMouse.jpg style="float: right; width: 10rem")

* "Design flaws" keep turning out to be **good design**: the "backward" retina needs its blood supply; Müller cells act as optical fibers
* Much apparent cruelty evolved **within** kinds – even creationists allow that
* Pain hierarchy (Murray): insects likely feel no pain; animals are not aware that **they** are in pain
* Death as ecosystem "scene-setting" for man's free response to God
* Answer: the first two points we share; but Scripture places the groaning of creation **after** the curse (Romans 8:20), not in God's "very good"

---

## Where We Part and What Is at Stake

![](pics/ScaleImbalance.jpg style="float: right; width: 7rem")

* With Craig: equivocation exposed, mechanisms inadequate, design detectable, the first cell miraculous
* Against Craig: he rests on a non-literal Genesis 1–11 – Deck 2 shows the historical days stand
* Common ancestry is where the evidence is **weakest** for the young-earth view – and it is also the easiest point for Craig to concede
* The stakes: **God's goodness** (death before sin) and **the gospel** (Adam and Christ)
* Science keeps pointing to design; the text decides the timeline

# Practical Takeaways

---

## Practical Takeaways

![](pics/GodCreation.jpg style="float: right; width: 8rem")

* Ask "which **evolution**?" – agree on change within kinds, then press on the mechanisms
* Know two examples well: the **flagellum** and the **Cambrian explosion**
* Common features point to **one Designer** (Romans 1:20)
* Be fair to Christians who hold theistic evolution – then ask what death, Adam, and the cross mean on their view
* Death is an **enemy**, not a method – and Christ has defeated it
